# Mexican Tinga-Style Tofu
## Week of October 18, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~25 minutes
**Total Time:** ~45 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

Smoky chipotle-tomato braised tofu. Crumbled and shredded tofu simmers in a sauce of chipotles in adobo, fire-roasted tomatoes, and onion until deeply flavored. You did chipotle bowls early on — tinga is smokier, deeper, and more concentrated. Traditionally a chicken dish, but crumbled tofu absorbs the sauce perfectly.

**Ingredient chain:** Uses leftover onions and canned tomatoes from last week's Sri Lankan Black Curry.

---

## Mise en Place

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 30 min, crumbled by hand into shreds (like pulled meat)

*Sauce base:*
- 2 large yellow onions (from last week), thinly sliced
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cans (14 oz each) fire-roasted diced tomatoes (or regular diced tomatoes from last week)
- 4–6 chipotles in adobo sauce + 3 tbsp of the adobo sauce (from 1 can)
- 1 tsp dried oregano (Mexican oregano preferred)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- Salt to taste

*Toppings:*
- 1 bunch fresh cilantro, chopped
- 4 limes, cut into wedges
- Pickled red onion: 1 red onion thinly sliced + 1/2 cup lime juice + pinch of salt (let sit 20 min)

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry jasmine rice

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice and prep pickled onion
Start **5 cups dry jasmine rice**. Combine **sliced red onion**, **lime juice**, and **salt** in a bowl. Set aside.

### Step 2: Fry the tofu (8 min)
Heat **3 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Add the **crumbled tofu** and spread into a single layer. Let it cook undisturbed for 3–4 minutes to get golden and crispy on the bottom, then stir and repeat. Remove to a plate.

### Step 3: Cook the onions (5 min)
Add **1 tbsp oil**. Reduce heat to medium. Add the **sliced onions** and cook 5 minutes until soft and starting to brown.

### Step 4: Build the sauce (15 min)
Add the **garlic**, **oregano**, and **cumin**. Stir 1 minute. Add the **diced tomatoes**, **chipotles in adobo**, and **adobo sauce**. Stir well, breaking up the chipotles. Simmer 10 minutes until the sauce reduces and thickens.

### Step 5: Braise the tofu (5 min)
Return the **tofu** to the wok. Stir to coat in the sauce. Simmer 5 more minutes. The tofu will absorb the smoky sauce. Season with **salt**.

### Step 6: Serve
Portion rice. Top with tinga tofu. Garnish with **cilantro**, **pickled red onion**, and **lime wedges**.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- This is one of those rare dishes where the tofu truly tastes meaty — the chipotle smoke and the shredded texture create a convincing pulled-pork analog
- Flavors intensify overnight as the tofu absorbs more sauce
- Keep pickled onions in a separate container — they stay crisp and bright all week
