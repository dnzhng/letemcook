# Indian Palak Tofu (Spinach Curry)
## Week of December 20, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~30 minutes
**Total Time:** ~50 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok + blender

---

## Overview

Tofu cubes in a vibrant green spinach-based curry with ginger, garlic, cumin, and a touch of cream. Rich, earthy, and deeply green. You've done tikka masala and chana masala — palak is lighter, brighter, and fresher. Good contrast to holiday heaviness.

**Ingredient chain:** Clean start. Five-spice carries from pantry to next week's Taiwanese Braised Soy Tofu.

---

## Mise en Place

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into 1-inch cubes

*Spinach puree (blanch, then blend):*
- 2 lbs fresh baby spinach (or 2 lbs frozen, thawed and drained)
- 1/2 cup water (for blending)

*Curry base:*
- 2 large yellow onions, finely diced
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- 2-inch piece ginger, minced
- 2 serrano peppers, minced
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1/2 cup heavy cream (or coconut cream)
- 2 tbsp butter (or oil)
- Salt to taste

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry basmati rice

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice
Start **5 cups dry basmati rice**.

### Step 2: Blanch and blend spinach (5 min)
If using fresh: blanch **2 lbs spinach** in boiling water for 1 minute. Drain and immediately plunge into ice water. Squeeze out excess water. Blend with **1/2 cup water** into a smooth puree. Set aside.

### Step 3: Sear the tofu (8 min)
Heat **3 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Fry the **tofu cubes** until golden, about 4 minutes per batch. Remove.

### Step 4: Cook the base (8 min)
Add **2 tbsp butter** to the wok. Reduce heat to medium. Add the **onions** and cook 5 minutes until soft. Add the **garlic**, **ginger**, **serrano peppers**, **cumin**, **coriander**, **garam masala**, and **turmeric**. Stir 2 minutes. Add the **diced tomatoes** and cook 3 minutes until broken down.

### Step 5: Add spinach and simmer (10 min)
Pour in the **spinach puree**. Stir well. Simmer 8–10 minutes until the sauce thickens and the raw spinach taste cooks out. Stir in the **cream**. Season with **salt**.

### Step 6: Finish
Fold in the **tofu cubes**. Simmer 2 more minutes. Serve over rice.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- The spinach will darken from bright to deep green overnight — flavor remains excellent
- The cream prevents the spinach from drying out during reheating
- This is one of the healthiest recipes in the rotation — loaded with iron from spinach
