# Japanese Yaki Udon with Tofu
## Week of December 6, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~25 minutes
**Total Time:** ~35 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

Thick chewy udon noodles stir-fried in a wok with tofu, cabbage, mushrooms, and a simple sauce of soy, mirin, and Worcestershire. A noodle week to break up rice. Quick, satisfying, and completely different from your teriyaki and miso dishes. The Worcestershire sauce is the unexpected MVP — it adds a deep, tangy umami.

**Ingredient chain:** Clean start. Cabbage and mushrooms are cheap and common. Soy sauce always carries.

---

## Mise en Place

*Noodles:*
- 2 lbs frozen udon noodles (or 1.5 lbs dried, cooked per package)

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into thick rectangular slices

*Vegetables:*
- 1/2 head green cabbage, cut into 1-inch pieces (about 6 cups)
- 1 lb shiitake mushrooms, stemmed and sliced (or cremini)
- 1 bunch scallions, cut into 2-inch lengths
- 6 cloves garlic, minced

*Yaki udon sauce (whisk together):*
- 5 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp mirin
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp black pepper

*Garnish:*
- Bonito flakes (optional)
- Toasted sesame seeds
- Pickled ginger

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prep noodles
If using frozen udon, run under warm water to separate. If dried, cook per package and drain.

### Step 2: Sear the tofu (6 min)
Heat **3 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Sear the **tofu slices** until golden on both sides, about 3 minutes per side. Remove and cut into strips.

### Step 3: Stir-fry vegetables (5 min)
Add **1 tbsp oil**. Add the **mushrooms** and cook 3 minutes until browned. Add the **cabbage** and **garlic**. Stir-fry 2 minutes until the cabbage is slightly wilted but still has crunch.

### Step 4: Combine and sauce (3 min)
Add the **udon noodles** and **tofu strips**. Pour the **sauce** over everything. Toss with tongs over high heat for 2–3 minutes until the sauce coats the noodles and the edges start to caramelize. Add the **scallions** and toss once more.

### Step 5: Serve
Portion into containers. Top with **sesame seeds** and **bonito flakes** (if using).

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Udon noodles hold their chewy texture remarkably well during storage — much better than rice noodles
- Reheat in a pan with a splash of water for best results (microwave works but pan is better)
- The Worcestershire deepens in flavor overnight
