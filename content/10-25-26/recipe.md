# Dan Dan Noodles with Tofu (担担面)
## Week of October 25, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~25 minutes
**Total Time:** ~35 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

Spicy, creamy, nutty noodles with crumbled tofu, tahini (or Chinese sesame paste), chili oil, Sichuan peppercorn, and preserved mustard greens (zha cai). The Sichuan holy trinity complete: mapo in July, kung pao in July, dan dan now. This is street food — fast, punchy, and deeply satisfying.

**Ingredient chain:** Clean start. Sesame paste/tahini and Sichuan peppercorn carry from your pantry. Scallions carry to next week's Korean Tteokbokki.

---

## Mise en Place

*Noodles:*
- 1.5 lbs dried Chinese wheat noodles (or fresh if available — lo mein, Shanghai noodles, or spaghetti)

*Tofu crumble:*
- 2 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, crumbled finely
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine

*Dan dan sauce (whisk together — make per bowl or in bulk):*
- 6 tbsp Chinese sesame paste or tahini
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp chili oil (with sediment — the crispy bits matter)
- 2 tbsp Chinkiang black vinegar
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp ground Sichuan peppercorn
- 4 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 1/2 cup hot noodle cooking water (added when assembling)

*Toppings:*
- 1/2 cup preserved mustard greens (zha cai), rinsed and chopped
- 4 scallions, sliced
- Crushed roasted peanuts
- Extra chili oil

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the tofu crumble (8 min)
Heat **2 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Add the **crumbled tofu**. Cook, stirring occasionally, until golden and crispy, about 6–8 minutes. Add **2 tbsp soy sauce** and **1 tbsp Shaoxing wine**. Stir 1 minute until absorbed. Remove.

### Step 2: Cook noodles (5 min)
Boil **1.5 lbs noodles** per package directions until just al dente. Reserve **1 cup cooking water** before draining.

### Step 3: Make the sauce
Whisk the **dan dan sauce** ingredients together. Add **1/2 cup hot noodle water** and whisk until smooth and pourable.

### Step 4: Assemble
Divide sauce among containers (about 3 tbsp per serving). Add drained noodles. Top with **tofu crumble**, **zha cai**, **scallions**, and **crushed peanuts**. Drizzle with extra **chili oil**.

### Step 5: Serve
Toss everything together before eating. The sauce coats the noodles in a creamy, spicy, nutty film.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- The noodles will absorb sauce overnight — pack a little extra sauce or add a splash of water when eating
- Toss noodles with 1 tsp sesame oil before storing to prevent sticking
- This works at room temperature too — don't need to reheat if you're in a rush
