# West African Peanut Stew with Tofu
## Week of August 2, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~30 minutes
**Total Time:** ~55 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

A creamy, deeply savory stew built from peanut butter, tomatoes, onion, garlic, and ginger. West African peanut stew is warming despite the summer heat — the peanut butter melts into a thick, velvety sauce that coats everything. No specialty ingredients needed. This is your first West African dish.

**Ingredient chain:** Uses leftover ginger from last week's Kung Pao. Tomatoes, ginger, and onion carry to next week's Ethiopian Misir Wot.

---

## Mise en Place

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into 1-inch cubes

*Aromatics (chop and set together):*
- 3 large yellow onions, diced
- 10 cloves garlic, minced
- 3-inch piece fresh ginger (leftover from kung pao), minced
- 2 tsp cayenne pepper (adjust to taste)
- 1 tbsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander

*Stew base (measure and set together):*
- 1 cup natural peanut butter (creamy, unsweetened)
- 2 cans (14 oz each) diced tomatoes
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 4 cups vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar

*Greens (prep last):*
- 1 large bunch kale or collard greens, stems removed, roughly chopped (about 6 cups)

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry jasmine rice

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice
Start **5 cups dry jasmine rice**.

### Step 2: Sear the tofu (8 min)
Heat **3 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Fry the **tofu cubes** in two batches until golden, about 4 minutes per batch. Remove to a plate.

### Step 3: Soften the aromatics (5 min)
Reduce heat to medium. Add **1 tbsp oil**. Add the **onions** and cook 4 minutes until softened. Add the **garlic**, **ginger**, **cayenne**, **cumin**, and **coriander**. Stir for 1 minute until fragrant.

### Step 4: Build the stew (20 min)
Add the **diced tomatoes**, **tomato paste**, and **vegetable stock**. Stir well. Add the **peanut butter** and whisk until fully dissolved. Add **2 tbsp soy sauce** and **1 tbsp brown sugar**. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to medium-low. Simmer 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened.

### Step 5: Add greens and tofu (5 min)
Stir in the **kale or collard greens**. Cook 3–4 minutes until wilted. Return the **tofu cubes** to the wok. Simmer 2 more minutes. Taste and adjust salt/cayenne.

### Step 6: Serve
Ladle over rice. Optionally top with crushed peanuts and a squeeze of lime.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- This stew is one of the best meal prep dishes — it thickens and improves dramatically overnight
- The peanut butter keeps everything moist during reheating
- If it's too thick after refrigerating, add a splash of water when reheating
