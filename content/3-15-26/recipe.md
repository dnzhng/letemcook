# Crispy Teriyaki Tofu with Rice
## Week of March 15, 2026

**Servings:** 5 (half-portion week)
**Active Time:** ~25 minutes
**Total Time:** ~55 minutes (includes oven time)
**Equipment:** Oven + baking sheet (tofu) + wok or pot (rice)

---

## Overview

Simple Japanese-inspired teriyaki tofu. The tofu gets crispy via high-heat oven baking rather than pan-frying — hands-off and no cornstarch needed. The teriyaki sauce reduces naturally into a sticky glaze. Adapted from Emilie Eats. Only ~12 ingredients, most of which are pantry staples.

---

## Ingredients (5 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 1.5 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 1.75 cups dry jasmine rice

**Aromatics**
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1-inch knob fresh ginger, grated

**Teriyaki Sauce**
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp mirin
- 2 tbsp sake (or dry sherry or rice vinegar)
- 1 tbsp sugar or honey
- 1 tsp sesame oil

**Garnish**
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- 2–3 green onions, sliced

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press 30+ min, then tear or cut into irregular 1-inch chunks. (Torn edges crisp up better in the oven than clean cuts.)
- **Preheat oven to 400°F.**
- **Make teriyaki sauce:** Whisk soy sauce + mirin + sake + sugar + sesame oil + garlic + ginger in a small bowl. Reserve half for glazing; use other half to toss tofu before baking.
- **Toss tofu in half the sauce** and spread on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
- **Cook rice** per package. Fluff and set aside.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook rice
Cook rice per package. Fluff and set aside.

### Step 2: Bake tofu (25–30 min)
Bake tofu at 400°F for 25–30 min, flipping once at the halfway point, until edges are golden and slightly crisp.

### Step 3: Reduce remaining sauce (3–4 min)
While tofu bakes, pour remaining sauce into a small saucepan or the wok over medium heat. Simmer 3–4 min until thickened and slightly syrupy. Watch closely — it reduces fast.

### Step 4: Glaze tofu
Remove tofu from oven. Toss immediately in reduced teriyaki glaze while hot.

### Step 5: Portion
Serve glazed tofu over rice. Top with sesame seeds and green onions.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days refrigerated.
- Reheat in microwave or briefly in a 375°F oven to re-crisp.
- The oven method is more hands-off than pan-frying — good for making rice simultaneously.

---

## To Scale to 10 Servings

- 3 blocks tofu
- 3.5 cups dry rice
- Double all sauce quantities
