# Cuban Black Beans & Rice (Moros y Cristianos) with Crispy Tofu
## Week of May 10, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~65 minutes (mostly hands-off simmering)
**Equipment:** Single wok with lid

---

## Overview

Moros y Cristianos ("Moors and Christians") — Cuba's beloved black beans and rice cooked together so the rice absorbs all the cumin-oregano seasoned bean liquid and turns a deep, gorgeous dark color. Topped with crispy cumin-paprika tofu. Two critical techniques: don't drain the beans (the liquid is the flavor), and add two tablespoons of vinegar at the end (a traditional Cuban touch that adds a subtle tang you can't quite place but would definitely miss). Very short grocery list. Estimated ~$1.20–1.80/serving.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine rice

**Beans**
- 3 cans black beans (15 oz each) *(do NOT drain — the liquid is the sauce)*

**Produce**
- 2 large yellow onions, diced
- 1 large green bell pepper, diced
- 10 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup vegetable broth

**Sofrito Spices**
- 2 tbsp cumin
- 1 tbsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 3 bay leaves
- Salt and pepper

**Finish**
- 2 tbsp white wine vinegar *(traditional Cuban touch — add at the very end)*
- 3 tbsp olive oil

**Tofu Spice Rub**
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Salt
- 2 tbsp olive oil

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press 20+ min, then cube into 1-inch pieces.
- **Make tofu spice rub:** Combine 1 tsp cumin + 1 tsp smoked paprika + 1 tsp garlic powder + salt + 2 tbsp olive oil. Toss tofu in rub and set aside.
- **Dice onions** and **dice bell pepper** — combine in one bowl (sofrito base).
- **Mince garlic** and set aside.
- **Open canned beans and DO NOT DRAIN** — set aside with liquid.
- **Measure spices** (cumin, oregano, paprika) into one small bowl.
- **Measure vinegar** and set near the stove for the finish.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Fry tofu (10–12 min)
Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in wok over medium-high. Add spice-rubbed tofu. Fry 3–4 min per side until deeply golden and slightly caramelized. Remove and set aside.

### Step 2: Make sofrito (8–10 min)
In the same wok over medium heat, add 1 tbsp olive oil. Add onions and bell pepper. Cook 6–8 min until softened and golden. Add garlic and cook 2 more min.

### Step 3: Add spices (1 min)
Add cumin + oregano + paprika + bay leaves. Stir constantly 1 min until fragrant and darkened.

### Step 4: Add beans with liquid and rice (1 min)
Add ALL three cans of black beans with ALL their liquid. Add vegetable broth. Stir to combine. Add dry rice and stir again.

### Step 5: Simmer (20–22 min)
Bring to a boil, then reduce to low. Cover tightly and simmer 20–22 min until rice has absorbed all liquid. Do not lift the lid while simmering.

### Step 6: Rest and finish
Remove from heat. Leave lid on 5 min. Remove bay leaves. Add 2 tbsp white wine vinegar and stir gently through the rice — this is a traditional Cuban touch. The rice should be deep black-purple and fragrant.

### Step 7: Combine and portion
Portion rice into containers. Top with crispy tofu. Taste and adjust salt.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days. Excellent reheated.
- The rice keeps its color and the beans stay intact.
- Reheat in microwave with a splash of water.
