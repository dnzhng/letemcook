# Ethiopian Misir Wot with Crispy Tofu
## Week of August 9, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~25 minutes
**Total Time:** ~55 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

A warmly spiced red lentil stew with berbere — Ethiopia's signature spice blend of chili, cumin, coriander, fenugreek, cardamom, and cinnamon. Earthy, deeply flavored, and comforting. Pan-fried tofu cubes added on top for extra protein and texture contrast. Completely new flavor universe.

**Ingredient chain:** Uses leftover tomatoes, ginger, and onions from last week's West African Peanut Stew. Tomatoes and onion carry to next week's Turkish Menemen.

---

## Mise en Place

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into 3/4-inch cubes

*Berbere spice blend (combine in a small bowl — or use 3 tbsp store-bought berbere):*
- 2 tbsp paprika
- 1 tbsp cayenne (reduce for less heat)
- 1 tsp ground cumin (from last week)
- 1 tsp ground coriander (from last week)
- 1/2 tsp ground fenugreek
- 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp ground cloves
- 1/4 tsp ground allspice

*Stew base:*
- 3 large yellow onions (from last week's buy), finely diced
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 2-inch piece ginger (from last week), minced
- 2 cans (14 oz each) diced tomatoes (from last week's buy)
- 2 tbsp tomato paste (from last week)
- 2.5 cups red lentils, rinsed
- 5 cups water or vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp neutral oil or butter (niter kibbeh if you have it)
- Salt to taste

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry jasmine or basmati rice

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice
Start **5 cups dry rice**.

### Step 2: Sear the tofu (8 min)
Heat **3 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Fry the **tofu cubes** in two batches until golden and crispy, about 4 minutes per batch. Remove to a plate.

### Step 3: Cook the onion base (10 min)
In the same wok, reduce heat to medium. Add **2 tbsp oil**. Add the **onions** and cook, stirring often, for 8–10 minutes until deeply golden and soft. This long cook is essential — Ethiopian stews start with heavily caramelized onions.

### Step 4: Add spices and aromatics (2 min)
Add the **garlic**, **ginger**, and **entire berbere spice blend**. Stir constantly for 1–2 minutes until very fragrant.

### Step 5: Build the stew (25 min)
Add the **diced tomatoes** and **tomato paste**. Stir and cook 3 minutes. Add the **red lentils** and **5 cups water/stock**. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Cook 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lentils are completely broken down and the stew is thick and creamy. Add **1.5 tsp salt** (adjust to taste).

### Step 6: Serve
Portion rice into containers. Ladle lentil stew over rice. Top with **crispy tofu cubes**.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Lentil stew thickens significantly in the fridge — add a splash of water when reheating
- The berbere flavor deepens overnight, making day 2–3 the best
- Keep tofu on top of the stew rather than submerged to preserve some crispness
- If you can find injera (Ethiopian flatbread) at a local market, it's the traditional accompaniment
