# Filipino Sisig-Style Tofu
## Week of September 13, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~30 minutes
**Total Time:** ~45 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

Crispy chopped tofu tossed with onions, chilies, calamansi (or lime), soy sauce, and mayo on a sizzling-hot plate. This is Filipino bar food — tangy, fatty, crunchy, and wildly addictive. Completely different from the vinegar-braised adobo you made in February. Traditionally served on a sizzling cast iron plate with an egg cracked on top.

**Ingredient chain:** Uses leftover soy sauce and onions from last week's Japchae. Lime carries to next week's Vietnamese Bún Noodles.

---

## Mise en Place

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 30 min (extra dry for maximum crispness), chopped into small 1/2-inch cubes

*Aromatics (chop together):*
- 3 medium yellow onions (from last week's buy), finely diced
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- 6 Thai chilies or 3 jalapeños, minced
- 1 bunch scallions, sliced

*Sauce (whisk together):*
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp calamansi juice (or lime juice — about 4 limes)
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp butter (for the sizzle plate effect)

*Optional:*
- 5 eggs (one per 2-serving portion, fried on top when serving)

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry jasmine rice

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice
Start **5 cups dry jasmine rice**.

### Step 2: Fry the tofu until extremely crispy (10 min)
Heat **4 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Add the **diced tofu** in a single layer (work in batches). Fry without stirring for 3–4 minutes to get a deep golden crust, then toss and continue frying until crispy on multiple sides, about 8–10 minutes total across batches. Remove to a plate.

### Step 3: Cook aromatics (3 min)
Drain excess oil, leaving about 2 tbsp. Add **1 tbsp butter**. Add the **onions** and cook 2 minutes. Add the **garlic** and **chilies**. Cook 1 minute.

### Step 4: Combine (2 min)
Return the **crispy tofu** to the wok. Pour in the **sauce mixture**. Toss aggressively over high heat for 1–2 minutes. The mayo will melt and create a creamy, tangy coating. Add the **scallions** and toss once more.

### Step 5: Serve
Portion rice into containers. Pile sisig tofu on top. If eating fresh, fry an egg and set it on top — break the yolk into the hot tofu.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- The tofu will lose some crispness overnight, but the flavor remains incredible
- For crispier reheats, spread on a sheet pan and broil for 3–4 minutes
- The mayo keeps things moist and adds richness without being noticeable as "mayo"
