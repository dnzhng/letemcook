# Korean Japchae (잡채)
## Week of September 6, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~50 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

Glass noodles (sweet potato starch noodles) stir-fried with tofu, spinach, mushrooms, and bell peppers in a sesame-soy-sugar glaze. Japchae is served warm or at room temperature, which makes it an exceptional meal prep dish. You've done gochujang bowls and kimchi jjigae, but never japchae — it's sweeter, more delicate, and noodle-based.

**Ingredient chain:** Clean start. Soy sauce and sesame carry to next week's Filipino Sisig. Buy extra mushrooms for both weeks.

---

## Mise en Place

*Noodles:*
- 1.5 lbs dried sweet potato glass noodles (dangmyeon)

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into thin strips

*Vegetables (slice and set in separate piles):*
- 1 lb fresh spinach
- 1 lb shiitake or cremini mushrooms, sliced
- 3 bell peppers (any color), cut into thin strips
- 2 medium yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 6 cloves garlic, minced

*Sauce (whisk together):*
- 6 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp sugar
- 3 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 2 tbsp mirin or rice wine
- 1 tsp black pepper

*Garnish:*
- 2 tbsp sesame seeds

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook noodles (5 min)
Boil **1.5 lbs glass noodles** according to package (usually 5–7 minutes). Drain and rinse with cold water. Cut with scissors into shorter lengths. Toss with **1 tbsp sesame oil** to prevent sticking.

### Step 2: Blanch spinach (2 min)
In the same water, blanch **1 lb spinach** for 30 seconds. Drain, squeeze out all water, and chop roughly. Toss with **1 tsp sesame oil** and **1 tsp soy sauce**.

### Step 3: Stir-fry tofu (6 min)
Heat **2 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Fry the **tofu strips** until golden, about 3 minutes per side. Remove.

### Step 4: Stir-fry vegetables (6 min)
Add **1 tbsp oil**. Stir-fry **mushrooms** for 3 minutes until browned. Remove. Add **1 tbsp oil**, stir-fry **bell peppers** and **onions** for 3 minutes until softened. Add **garlic** for 30 seconds. Remove.

### Step 5: Combine (3 min)
Return everything to the wok: **noodles**, **tofu**, **mushrooms**, **bell peppers/onions**, and **spinach**. Pour in the **sauce**. Toss over medium heat for 2–3 minutes until everything is coated and the sauce is absorbed.

### Step 6: Finish
Sprinkle with **sesame seeds**. Serve warm or at room temperature.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Japchae is one of the few dishes that tastes great at room temperature — no reheating needed
- The noodles absorb sauce overnight; you can toss with a bit more soy sauce + sesame oil before eating
- Glass noodles have a uniquely bouncy, chewy texture that holds up well over several days
