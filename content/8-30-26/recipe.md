# Japanese Curry Tofu (カレーライス)
## Week of August 30, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~20 minutes
**Total Time:** ~50 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

Rich, mild, slightly sweet Japanese curry — nothing like Indian or Thai curry. The S&B Golden Curry roux blocks do all the heavy lifting (no cornstarch, no individual spices to measure). Crispy pan-fried tofu over rice with thick, glossy curry ladled on top. Potatoes and onions round it out. This is comfort food perfection and one of the easiest recipes in the rotation.

**Ingredient chain:** Uses leftover potatoes and onions from last week's Lomo Saltado.

---

## Mise en Place

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into 1-inch cubes

*Vegetables:*
- 2 large yellow onions (from last week's buy), sliced into half-moons
- 2 lbs potatoes (from last week's buy), peeled and cut into 1-inch chunks
- 2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into 1-inch chunks (optional)

*Curry:*
- 2 boxes S&B Golden Curry roux (medium hot), about 16 portions worth
- 6 cups water
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey or brown sugar
- 1 tbsp neutral oil

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry Japanese short-grain rice (or jasmine)

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice
Start **5 cups dry rice**.

### Step 2: Sear the tofu (8 min)
Heat **3 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Fry the **tofu cubes** in two batches until golden, about 4 minutes per batch. Remove to a plate.

### Step 3: Cook the vegetables (10 min)
Add **1 tbsp oil** to the wok. Reduce heat to medium. Add the **onions** and cook 5 minutes until soft and translucent. Add the **potatoes** (and sweet potatoes if using). Stir to coat in oil.

### Step 4: Simmer (15 min)
Add **6 cups water**. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Cook 12–15 minutes until the potatoes are fork-tender.

### Step 5: Add the roux (5 min)
Turn off the heat. Break the **curry roux blocks** into the wok. Stir until completely dissolved. Turn heat back to low and simmer 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened. Add **2 tbsp soy sauce** and **1 tbsp honey**. Taste and adjust.

### Step 6: Serve
Portion rice into containers. Ladle curry over one side. Place **crispy tofu** on top.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Japanese curry is the ultimate meal prep food — it thickens and improves every single day
- The potatoes absorb flavor overnight and become incredibly soft and rich
- Add a splash of water when reheating to loosen
- The tofu will soften in the curry but that's how it's traditionally served
