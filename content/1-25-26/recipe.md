# Thai Basil Tofu Stir-Fry (Pad Krapow)
## Week of January 25, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~30 minutes
**Total Time:** ~40 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok

---

## Overview

Pad Krapow is one of Thailand's most beloved street foods — a fast, high-heat stir-fry of garlic, chilies, and holy basil (or Thai basil) with a savory sauce. This version uses crumbled or cubed tofu in place of ground pork. The key move is using a screaming hot wok and adding basil off-heat so it stays bright. Adapted from Drive Me Hungry and Plant Based Jess.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine rice

**Aromatics**
- 12 cloves garlic, minced
- 6–8 Thai bird's eye chilies, thinly sliced (adjust to heat tolerance)

**Sauce**
- 4 tbsp oyster sauce (or vegetarian oyster sauce)
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp fish sauce (or 2 extra tbsp soy sauce for vegan)
- 2 tsp sugar
- 2 tbsp neutral oil

**Basil**
- 2 large bunches fresh Thai basil (holy basil if available) — leaves only

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press 20+ min. For Pad Krapow, crumble tofu into rough chunks (like ground meat texture) rather than cubing — this gives better texture and more surface area to crisp.
- **Mince garlic** and **slice chilies** — keep together in one bowl. This is a very garlic-forward dish; don't skimp.
- **Mix sauce:** Stir together oyster sauce + soy sauce + fish sauce + sugar in a small bowl. Set aside.
- **Pick basil leaves** off stems — keep in a bowl near the stove. Do NOT add until the wok is off the heat.
- **Cook rice** per package. Fluff and set aside.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook rice
Cook rice per package. Fluff and set aside.

### Step 2: Fry tofu (8–10 min)
Heat 2 tbsp oil in wok over high heat until nearly smoking. Add crumbled tofu. Press against wok surface and let sit 2–3 min without stirring until golden. Stir, press again, and repeat until most pieces are crispy and golden. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Fry garlic and chilies (45 sec)
In the same wok over high heat, add garlic and chilies. Stir-fry 30–45 seconds — fast, fragrant, and just starting to color.

### Step 4: Sauce and return tofu (2 min)
Pour sauce into wok. Stir to combine with the garlic. Return tofu. Toss over high heat 1–2 min until everything is coated and sauce has reduced slightly.

### Step 5: Add basil OFF HEAT
Remove wok from heat. Add all the Thai basil leaves. Toss quickly — the residual heat wilts the basil perfectly. Adding on heat turns it black and bitter.

### Step 6: Portion
Serve over rice immediately.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days refrigerated.
- Basil will darken in storage — this is normal; flavor stays.
- Reheat in microwave or briefly back in the wok.
- Add a fried egg on top when serving if desired (traditional in Thailand).
