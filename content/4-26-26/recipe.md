# Century Egg & Shrimp Congee + Ginger Scallion Tofu over Rice
## Week of April 26, 2026

**Servings:** 10 total — 2 servings congee + 8 servings ginger scallion tofu
**Active Time:** ~45 minutes
**Total Time:** ~70 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok + pot for congee

---

## Overview

A two-dish week designed to use up leftover century eggs and frozen shrimp while the main dish (ginger scallion tofu) carries the bulk of the servings. The grocery lists overlap almost entirely, keeping the shopping run minimal.

---

# DISH 1: Century Egg & Shrimp Congee (2 servings)

## Ingredients

- 1 cup dry jasmine rice
- 2 century eggs (皮蛋), peeled and quartered
- Small amount frozen shrimp, peeled and deveined (leftover)
- 6 cups vegetable or chicken broth
- 1-inch knob fresh ginger, julienned
- 2 green onions, sliced
- Sesame oil — drizzle
- White pepper — pinch

## Mise en Place (Congee)

- **Rinse rice** once.
- **Peel and quarter century eggs** — they'll be dark and gelatinous; this is correct.
- **Thaw shrimp** if frozen.
- **Julienne ginger.**
- **Measure broth.**

## Steps (Congee)

### Step 1: Simmer rice (45–50 min)
Combine rice + 6 cups broth in a pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer. Cook uncovered 40–50 min, stirring occasionally, until rice has broken down into a thick, creamy porridge. Add more water if it gets too thick.

### Step 2: Add ginger, shrimp, and eggs (5 min)
Add julienned ginger. Add shrimp — they cook in 2–3 min. Add century egg pieces. Stir gently.

### Step 3: Finish
Season with white pepper and salt. Drizzle sesame oil. Top with green onions. Serve immediately — congee thickens as it sits; add a splash of hot water when reheating.

# DISH 2: Ginger Scallion Tofu over Rice (8 servings)

## Ingredients

- 2.5 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 3 cups dry jasmine rice

**Aromatics**
- 3 bunches green onions *(go very heavy — the scallion oil is the whole sauce)*
- 3-inch knob fresh ginger, julienned or minced
- 8 cloves garlic, minced

**Sauce**
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp sesame oil (toasted)
- ½ cup + 2 tbsp neutral oil (for frying tofu AND for making the scallion oil)
- 1 tsp sugar

## Mise en Place (Ginger Scallion Tofu)

- **Press tofu:** Press 20+ min, then cube into ¾-inch pieces. Pat very dry.
- **Thinly slice all green onions** — keep in a large heatproof bowl. This is your sauce vessel.
- **Julienne or finely mince ginger** and add to the green onions.
- **Mince garlic** and add to the bowl.
- **Mix soy + sesame oil + sugar** in a small bowl. Set aside.
- **Cook rice** per package. Fluff and set aside.

## Steps (Ginger Scallion Tofu)

### Step 1: Cook rice
Cook rice per package. Fluff and set aside.

### Step 2: Fry tofu (8–10 min)
Heat 2 tbsp oil in wok over medium-high. Fry tofu 3–4 min per side until golden and crispy on all sides. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Make scallion oil — the key step
In the same wok, heat ½ cup neutral oil over high heat until shimmering and nearly smoking. Pour the HOT oil directly over the bowl of sliced green onions and ginger. It will sizzle and spit violently — this is correct. The heat blooms the aromatics instantly.

### Step 4: Add soy mixture
Pour the soy + sesame oil + sugar into the scallion-ginger bowl. Stir to combine. This is your sauce.

### Step 5: Combine
Pour the scallion sauce over the fried tofu. Toss to coat.

### Step 6: Portion
Serve over rice. Add garlic (raw, on top) or fry garlic briefly in a bit of oil first if you prefer milder flavor.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- **Congee:** Thickens significantly in the fridge. Reheat with a generous splash of water and stir well.
- **Ginger scallion tofu:** Stores 5 days. The scallion oil is intensely flavorful and keeps well.
- Keep congee and ginger scallion tofu in separate containers for the week.
