# Indian Chana Masala with Crispy Tofu
## Week of October 4, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~30 minutes
**Total Time:** ~55 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

Chickpeas simmered in a spiced tomato-onion sauce with cumin, coriander, garam masala, and amchoor (dried mango powder for tang). Pan-fried tofu cubes added on top for extra protein. You did tikka masala back in December — chana masala is drier, tangier, and more rustic. A North Indian staple.

**Ingredient chain:** Chickpea theme from last week's Burmese Thoke. Tomatoes and onions carry to next week's Sri Lankan Black Curry.

---

## Mise en Place

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into 3/4-inch cubes

*Chickpeas:*
- 4 cans (15 oz each) chickpeas, drained and rinsed

*Aromatics:*
- 3 large yellow onions, finely diced
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- 2-inch piece ginger, minced
- 2 serrano or jalapeño peppers, minced

*Spices (measure into a small bowl):*
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1.5 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp amchoor (dried mango powder) — or sub 2 tbsp lemon juice at the end
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp cayenne

*Sauce:*
- 2 cans (14 oz each) diced tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 cup water
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 bunch fresh cilantro, chopped

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry basmati rice

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice
Start **5 cups dry basmati rice**.

### Step 2: Sear the tofu (8 min)
Heat **3 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Fry the **tofu cubes** in two batches until golden, about 4 minutes per batch. Remove to a plate.

### Step 3: Cook the onion base (8 min)
Add **2 tbsp oil** to the wok. Reduce heat to medium. Add the **onions** and cook 6–8 minutes until deeply golden.

### Step 4: Add spices and aromatics (2 min)
Add the **garlic**, **ginger**, **chilies**, and the **entire spice bowl**. Stir constantly for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.

### Step 5: Build the curry (20 min)
Add the **diced tomatoes** and **tomato paste**. Cook 5 minutes, breaking down the tomatoes. Add the **chickpeas** and **1 cup water**. Add **1 tsp salt**. Bring to a simmer and cook 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens and coats the chickpeas. Lightly mash a few chickpeas against the side of the wok to thicken the sauce further.

### Step 6: Finish
Stir in **amchoor** (or lemon juice). Fold in the **crispy tofu** and half the **cilantro**. Serve over rice, garnished with remaining cilantro.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- The chickpeas absorb the spices overnight and taste even better on day 2–3
- The tofu softens in the sauce but keeps its shape well
- Add a squeeze of lemon when reheating to brighten the flavors
