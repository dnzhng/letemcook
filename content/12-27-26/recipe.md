# Taiwanese Braised Soy Tofu (滷豆腐)
## Week of December 27, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~20 minutes
**Total Time:** ~70 minutes (mostly hands-off braising)
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

A slow-braised master sauce (lu) of soy sauce, rock sugar, five-spice, star anise, cinnamon, and Shaoxing wine. Tofu and hard-boiled eggs simmer until deeply infused with the dark, aromatic liquid. You did three cup tofu in March — this is the other iconic Taiwanese tofu preparation. A perfect contemplative end-of-year dish.

**Ingredient chain:** Uses five-spice from pantry (same jar since November). Last recipe of the year — no carry needed.

---

## Mise en Place

*Tofu:*
- 4 blocks firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into thick triangles or large rectangles

*Master braising liquid (lu):*
- 1 cup soy sauce
- 1/2 cup Shaoxing wine
- 4 cups water
- 3 tbsp rock sugar (or brown sugar)
- 1 tbsp Chinese five-spice powder
- 5 star anise
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 4 cloves
- 3-inch piece ginger, sliced
- 6 cloves garlic, smashed
- 2 scallions, cut into 3-inch lengths
- 2 dried bay leaves

*Add-ins:*
- 10 hard-boiled eggs, peeled
- 1 lb firm pressed tofu (dougan) or additional regular tofu

*Greens:*
- 2 lbs bok choy or gai lan, blanched in salted water

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry jasmine rice

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice
Start **5 cups dry jasmine rice**.

### Step 2: Pan-fry the tofu (8 min)
Heat **3 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Fry the **tofu pieces** until golden on both sides, about 4 minutes per side. This step adds texture and helps the tofu absorb the braising liquid. Remove.

### Step 3: Build the master sauce (5 min)
In the same wok, add **1 tbsp oil**. Over medium heat, add the **ginger**, **garlic**, and **scallions**. Stir 1 minute. Add all the **braising liquid ingredients**: soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, water, rock sugar, five-spice, star anise, cinnamon sticks, cloves, and bay leaves. Bring to a boil, stirring to dissolve the sugar.

### Step 4: Braise (45 min)
Reduce to a gentle simmer. Add the **fried tofu** and **peeled hard-boiled eggs**. The liquid should mostly cover everything — add more water if needed. Cover and simmer on low heat for 40–45 minutes, turning the tofu and eggs once halfway through. The liquid will reduce and become a dark, syrupy sauce.

### Step 5: Blanch the greens (3 min)
While braising, blanch **2 lbs bok choy** in salted boiling water for 2–3 minutes until bright green and just tender. Drain.

### Step 6: Serve
Portion rice. Arrange **braised tofu** and a **halved egg** on top. Drizzle with braising liquid. Serve **blanched greens** on the side.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- The master sauce is a living thing in Taiwanese cooking — traditionally it's kept and reused, getting better each time
- The tofu and eggs absorb more sauce every day, becoming darker and more flavorful
- Day 3–4 portions are the best — the eggs become "tea egg" colored all the way through
- This is one of the most effortless recipes despite the long cook time — it's 90% passive simmering
- A fitting end to the year: simple ingredients, deep flavors, patience rewarded
