# Spanish Smoky Paprika Tofu with Tomato Rice
## Week of March 29, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~40 minutes
**Total Time:** ~65 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok with lid

---

## Overview

Crispy tofu rubbed in smoked paprika (pimentón) served over tomato rice — rice that absorbs a cumin-seasoned tomato broth and turns a deep, gorgeous color. Inspired by Spain on a Fork's oven-roasted tofu and traditional Spanish arroz con tomate. The star ingredient is Spanish smoked paprika, which gives the tofu an earthy, smoky depth that's completely unique. A total departure from the Asian rotation.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine rice

**Produce**
- 2 large yellow onions, diced
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 red bell peppers, diced
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 2 cups vegetable broth

**Tofu Spice Rub**
- 2 tbsp smoked paprika (pimentón) *(star ingredient — use more than seems right)*
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp olive oil

**Rice Seasonings**
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and pepper

**For cooking**
- 3 tbsp olive oil total

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press 20+ min, then slice into ¾-inch slabs or cubes.
- **Make spice rub:** Combine 2 tbsp smoked paprika + garlic powder + onion powder + 1 tsp cumin + salt + 2 tbsp olive oil into a paste. Coat tofu slabs and let marinate while you prep everything else.
- **Dice onions** and **mince garlic** — keep separate.
- **Dice bell peppers** and set aside.
- **Open crushed tomatoes** and **measure broth** — combine in one bowl.
- **Measure rice seasonings** (paprika, cumin, oregano) — combine in a small bowl.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Sear tofu (10–12 min)
Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in wok over medium-high. Add marinated tofu. Sear 4–5 min per side without moving — you want a dark, caramelized crust from the paprika. Work in batches. Remove and set aside.

### Step 2: Build rice base (8–10 min)
In the same wok over medium heat, add 1 tbsp olive oil. Add onions. Cook 5 min until soft. Add garlic. Cook 2 min. Add bell peppers. Cook 2 more min.

### Step 3: Toast rice and spices (2 min)
Add dry rice to the wok. Stir to coat in the oil and vegetables. Add rice seasonings and toast 1–2 min, stirring, until rice smells nutty.

### Step 4: Simmer rice (18–20 min)
Add crushed tomatoes + vegetable broth. Stir well. Bring to a boil, then reduce to low. Cover and simmer 18–20 min until rice has absorbed all liquid. Do not lift the lid while simmering.

### Step 5: Rest and fluff (5 min)
Remove from heat. Leave lid on for 5 min. Then fluff rice with a fork — it should be deeply colored and fragrant.

### Step 6: Combine and portion
Nestle tofu pieces into the rice or portion separately. Taste and adjust salt and smoked paprika.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days refrigerated.
- Rice keeps its texture well when reheated.
- The smoked paprika flavor deepens as it sits.
