# Twice-Cooked Pork Belly (回锅肉 Hui Guo Rou)
## Week of March 22, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~60 minutes (includes par-boiling)
**Equipment:** Single wok + pot for par-boiling

---

## Overview

One of the most famous dishes in Sichuan cuisine. "Twice-cooked" means the pork is first par-boiled whole, then sliced thin and returned to the wok with doubanjiang (spicy broad bean paste), fermented black beans, and aromatics. The pork fat renders into the sauce and creates an incredibly rich, spicy, savory result. Adapted from The Woks of Life. Green beans used this week as an exception to the usual exclusion.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 1–1.5 lbs pork belly (leftover or purchased)
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine rice

**Produce**
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- 2-inch knob fresh ginger, minced
- 1 bunch green onions/scallions, sliced
- Long hot green peppers — 3–4, sliced (optional, traditional)
- Green beans — 2 lbs (exception week)

**Key Sauces**
- 3 tbsp doubanjiang / Pixian spicy broad bean paste *(essential — find at Asian grocery)*
- 2 tbsp fermented black beans (douchi) — optional but adds depth
- 3 tbsp Shaoxing wine
- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp sugar

---

## Mise en Place

- **Par-boil pork belly:** Place whole pork belly in a pot, cover with cold water + a few slices ginger + 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine. Bring to a boil, skim foam, simmer 20–25 min until just cooked through. Remove and cool. Slice thin (¼-inch) when cool enough to handle.
- **Mince garlic and ginger** — combine in one bowl.
- **Slice green onions** — keep whites and greens separate.
- **Slice peppers** (if using).
- **Trim green beans** and blanch in boiling water 2 min; drain and set aside.
- **Measure sauces** — have doubanjiang, black beans, Shaoxing, dark soy, light soy, and sugar all ready at the stove.
- **Cook rice** per package. Fluff and set aside.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook rice
Cook rice per package. Fluff and set aside.

### Step 2: Par-boil pork belly (25 min)
If not already done: boil pork belly with ginger and Shaoxing wine until just cooked. Cool and slice thin.

### Step 3: Render pork belly in wok (6–8 min)
Heat wok over medium. Add pork belly slices — no oil. The fat will render. Cook until slices are lightly browned and curled at edges. Remove and set aside. Leave fat in wok.

### Step 4: Fry doubanjiang (1–2 min)
Over medium-high heat, add doubanjiang to the rendered pork fat. Stir-fry 1–2 min until darkened and fragrant — the chili oil will turn deep red.

### Step 5: Add aromatics (1 min)
Add garlic, ginger, and green onion whites. Stir-fry 45 seconds.

### Step 6: Return pork and add sauce (2–3 min)
Return pork belly. Add fermented black beans (if using) + Shaoxing wine + dark soy + light soy + sugar. Toss and stir-fry over high heat 2–3 min.

### Step 7: Add green beans (2 min)
Add blanched green beans. Toss everything together 1–2 min.

### Step 8: Finish
Add green onion greens and sliced peppers. Toss. Remove from heat.

### Step 9: Portion
Serve over rice.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 4 days refrigerated.
- Reheat in the wok over high heat — brings back the wok hei.
- The pork fat is the flavor; don't trim it.
