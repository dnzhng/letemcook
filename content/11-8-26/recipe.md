# Thai Massaman Curry Tofu
## Week of November 8, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~25 minutes
**Total Time:** ~55 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

The mellowest Thai curry — warm spices like cinnamon, cardamom, star anise, and cloves plus peanuts, potatoes, and coconut milk. Muslim-influenced, sweet, rich, and fragrant. Completely different from your green and red curries. This is the one Thai curry that uses "warm" spices rather than "bright" ones.

**Ingredient chain:** Clean start. Potatoes carry to... actually, just buy what you need. Onions carry to next week's Lebanese Mujaddara.

---

## Mise en Place

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into 1-inch cubes

*Vegetables:*
- 2 lbs baby potatoes (or Yukon gold), halved
- 2 large yellow onions, cut into wedges
- 1 cup roasted peanuts

*Curry:*
- 5 tbsp Massaman curry paste (Mae Ploy or Maesri)
- 3 cans (13.5 oz each) coconut milk
- 3 tbsp fish sauce
- 3 tbsp palm sugar or brown sugar
- 2 tbsp tamarind paste (or 1 tbsp lime juice + 1 tbsp brown sugar)
- 3 bay leaves
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 3 cardamom pods, lightly crushed
- 1 star anise

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry jasmine rice

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice
Start **5 cups dry jasmine rice**.

### Step 2: Sear the tofu (8 min)
Heat **3 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Fry the **tofu cubes** until golden, about 4 minutes per batch. Remove.

### Step 3: Bloom the paste (3 min)
Reduce heat to medium. Spoon the thick cream from **1 can coconut milk** into the wok. Add **5 tbsp Massaman paste**. Stir-fry 2–3 minutes until fragrant and oil separates.

### Step 4: Build the curry (25 min)
Add the remaining **coconut milk**, **potatoes**, **onion wedges**, **bay leaves**, **cinnamon stick**, **cardamom**, and **star anise**. Bring to a simmer. Cook 20–25 minutes until potatoes are fork-tender. Add **3 tbsp fish sauce**, **3 tbsp sugar**, and **2 tbsp tamarind paste**.

### Step 5: Finish
Return the **tofu** and add the **peanuts**. Simmer 3 more minutes. Remove whole spices before portioning.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Massaman is arguably the best Thai curry for meal prep — it's designed to be rich and mellow
- The potatoes absorb incredible flavor overnight
- The whole spices (cinnamon stick, star anise, cardamom) should be removed before storing
