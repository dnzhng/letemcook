# Vietnamese Caramelized Lemongrass Tofu (Đậu Hũ Xào Sả Ớt)
## Week of May 17, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~50 minutes (includes 30 min tofu press)
**Equipment:** Single wok

---

## Overview

A Southern Vietnamese street food classic — crispy pan-fried tofu tossed in a fragrant, caramelized sauce of lemongrass, shallots, garlic, and Thai chilies. Only 4–5 sauce ingredients but produces something deeply aromatic and complex. The sugar caramelizes naturally and coats every cube — no cornstarch. A total reset after weeks of heavy stews and curries. Serve over jasmine rice; finish with scallions and lime.

**Variation note:** Ground pork can be added after shallots soften. Broccoli can be added with the tofu in the final step, covered 2 min to steam.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each, ~2.75 lbs total) — press 30+ min, ideally overnight
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine rice

**Aromatics**
- 5 stalks fresh lemongrass, inner white/pale part only, very finely minced *(key purchase)*
- 5 shallots, finely minced
- 10 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 4–6 Thai bird's eye chilies, thinly sliced (adjust to heat)

**Sauce**
- 3 tbsp granulated sugar *(for caramelizing)*
- 3 tbsp soy sauce (light or dark)
- 2 tbsp fish sauce or soy sauce substitute
- 2 tbsp water

**For cooking**
- 4–5 tbsp neutral oil

**Garnish**
- 1 bunch green onions, sliced
- 2 limes, cut into wedges

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press at least 30 min — overnight in the fridge is ideal. Cut into ¾-inch cubes. Pat very dry with paper towels.
- **Mince lemongrass:** Peel off tough outer layers, use only the soft inner white/pale part. Mince extremely fine — lemongrass is fibrous and needs to be fine or it's unpleasant to eat.
- **Mince shallots** and **mince garlic** — combine with lemongrass in one bowl. This is your aromatic paste.
- **Slice chilies** and add to aromatic bowl.
- **Combine soy sauce + fish sauce + water** in a small bowl. Set aside.
- **Measure sugar separately** — it goes into the wok alone first.
- **Slice green onions** and cut lime wedges for serving.
- **Cook rice** per package. Fluff and set aside.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook rice
Cook rice per package. Fluff and set aside.

### Step 2: Fry tofu (10–12 min)
Heat 3 tbsp oil in wok over medium-high. Add tofu cubes in a single layer (work in batches). Fry undisturbed 4 min per side until deeply golden and crispy. Remove and set aside. Tofu must be very dry and very hot for best crust.

### Step 3: Fry aromatic paste (3–4 min)
Add 1–2 tbsp oil to wok over medium heat. Add lemongrass-shallot-garlic-chili mixture. Stir-fry 3–4 min until very fragrant and starting to turn golden. The lemongrass should soften and become translucent.

### Step 4: Caramelize sugar (2–3 min) ⚠️
Push aromatics to the side. Add 3 tbsp sugar to the center of the wok. Let it melt over medium heat without stirring — swirl the pan only. Watch closely; it burns fast. Once it turns amber (30–60 seconds once melted), immediately stir the caramel into the aromatics.

### Step 5: Add sauce and tofu (2–3 min)
Add soy sauce mixture. Stir well — it will bubble and spit. Return tofu. Toss over medium-high heat 2–3 min until every cube is coated in the caramelized glaze.

### Step 6: Finish and portion
Remove from heat. Taste — adjust with more soy sauce, fish sauce, or a pinch of sugar. Serve over rice. Top with green onions and lime wedges.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days. Flavor improves as tofu absorbs the caramelized sauce.
- Press tofu as long as possible — well-pressed tofu gives a dramatically crispier result.
- Reheat in microwave or briefly back in the wok.
