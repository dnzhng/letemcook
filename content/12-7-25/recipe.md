# Tofu Fried Rice
## Week of December 7, 2025

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~25 minutes
**Total Time:** ~30 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok

---

## Overview

A classic one-wok meal prep staple. Day-old cold rice is essential — it fries instead of steaming, giving each grain a light crisp. Tofu stands in for the traditional egg-and-meat combo, keeping it cheap and easy. Simple, endlessly reliable.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 2 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 8–10 cups cooked jasmine rice (cook 4 cups dry, ideally 1–2 days ahead)

**Produce**
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 bunch green onions, sliced (whites and greens separated)
- 2 cups frozen peas (or substitute: cabbage, edamame, or other quick-cooking veg)
- 4 eggs

**Sauce**
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (for tofu)
- 2 tbsp neutral oil (for rice)

---

## Mise en Place

Do these steps before you start cooking:

- **Press tofu:** Wrap blocks in a clean towel, place something heavy on top, press for at least 20 minutes. Cut into ¾-inch cubes.
- **Cook rice:** If not already done, cook rice and spread on a tray to cool completely (or refrigerate overnight uncovered).
- **Mince garlic** and set aside.
- **Slice green onions:** Keep whites and greens in separate piles.
- **Measure sauce:** Combine 4 tbsp soy sauce + 2 tsp sesame oil in a small bowl.
- **Beat eggs** in a small bowl.
- **Measure frozen peas** — keep frozen until needed.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Fry tofu (8–10 min)
Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in wok over medium-high. Add tofu cubes in a single layer. Let sit undisturbed 3–4 min until golden, then flip and repeat on other sides. Season with a splash of soy sauce. Remove and set aside.

### Step 2: Scramble eggs (2 min)
Add a small drizzle of oil to wok. Pour in beaten eggs and scramble until just set. Remove and set aside with the tofu.

### Step 3: Fry aromatics (1 min)
Add 2 tbsp oil to wok over high heat. Add garlic and green onion whites. Stir-fry 30–60 seconds until fragrant.

### Step 4: Fry rice (4–5 min)
Add cold rice. Press and toss with a spatula, breaking up any clumps. Let it sit against the wok surface for 1 min at a time to get some color. Repeat 3–4 times.

### Step 5: Sauce and combine (2 min)
Pour sauce over rice and toss to coat evenly. Add frozen peas and stir-fry 1–2 min until heated through. Return tofu and eggs. Toss everything together.

### Step 6: Finish
Add green onion greens. Taste and adjust soy sauce. Remove from heat.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores in the fridge up to 5 days in airtight containers.
- Reheat in microwave 90 seconds, or back in the wok with a splash of water.
- Rice will firm up in the fridge — a small splash of water before reheating helps.

---

## Substitutions

- **Frozen peas/carrots:** Swap for cabbage (thinly sliced), edamame, broccoli florets, or any quick-cooking vegetable.
- **Eggs:** Can omit for fully vegan version; add an extra half block of tofu.
