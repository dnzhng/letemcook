# Korean Tteokbokki-Inspired Tofu (떡볶이)
## Week of November 1, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~25 minutes
**Total Time:** ~40 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

Chewy rice cakes and crispy tofu in a sweet-spicy gochujang sauce. You've done gochujang bowls twice — this one uses the same paste but in a completely different format: saucy, sticky, almost like a braise with those bouncy rice cakes. Korean street food comfort for when it gets cold.

**Ingredient chain:** Uses leftover scallions from last week's Dan Dan Noodles. Gochujang carries to pantry.

---

## Mise en Place

*Rice cakes:*
- 2 lbs Korean rice cakes (tteok) — frozen or refrigerated, soaked in water 20 min if frozen

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into 1-inch cubes

*Sauce (whisk together):*
- 4 tbsp gochujang
- 2 tbsp gochugaru (Korean chili flakes) — or more gochujang if you can't find it
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp rice syrup or honey (for that glossy finish)
- 4 cups anchovy or vegetable stock (or water)

*Add-ins:*
- 1 bunch scallions (from last week), cut into 2-inch lengths
- 4 fish cakes (eomuk), sliced — optional but traditional
- 3 hard-boiled eggs, halved — optional
- 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds

*Rice:*
- 4 cups dry jasmine rice (less than usual — the rice cakes are the starch)

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice
Start **4 cups dry jasmine rice**.

### Step 2: Sear the tofu (8 min)
Heat **3 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Fry the **tofu cubes** until golden, about 4 minutes per batch. Remove.

### Step 3: Build the sauce (5 min)
Add the **sauce mixture** to the wok. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Stir until the gochujang dissolves completely.

### Step 4: Cook the rice cakes (8 min)
Add the **rice cakes** (and fish cakes if using). Simmer 6–8 minutes, stirring frequently, until the rice cakes are soft and chewy and the sauce has thickened into a glossy glaze.

### Step 5: Finish
Return the **tofu** to the wok. Add the **scallion lengths**. Toss everything together for 1–2 minutes. The sauce should coat everything in a thick, shiny layer. Sprinkle with **sesame seeds**.

### Step 6: Serve
Serve the tteokbokki alongside rice. Add halved boiled eggs if desired.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Rice cakes harden in the fridge — this is normal. They soften again perfectly when reheated with a splash of water
- Microwave with a wet paper towel on top, or reheat in a pan with 2 tbsp water
- The sauce thickens significantly when cold — it will return to glossy when reheated
