# Mapo Tofu (麻婆豆腐)
## Week of July 19, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~25 minutes
**Total Time:** ~40 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

The most famous tofu dish in the world — silky soft tofu swimming in a fiery, numbing sauce of doubanjiang, Sichuan peppercorn, and fermented black beans. This is the real Sichuan version: deeply savory, tingling with má là (numbing-spicy), and rich with rendered pork fat. You've done black pepper tofu and dry-fried broccoli from the Sichuan canon, but mapo is the cornerstone.

**Ingredient chain:** Doubanjiang, Sichuan peppercorn, and scallions carry to next week's Kung Pao Tofu.

---

## Mise en Place

*Tofu (do NOT press — you want it soft):*
- 3 blocks soft or medium-firm tofu, cut into 3/4-inch cubes
- Bring a pot of salted water to a gentle simmer, slide tofu in for 3 minutes to firm slightly, drain carefully

*Aromatics (prep and set together):*
- 1.5 lbs ground pork (or crumbled firm tofu for vegetarian)
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- 2-inch piece ginger, minced
- 1 bunch scallions, whites and greens separated, thinly sliced

*Sauce (measure and combine in a bowl):*
- 4 tbsp doubanjiang (Pixian brand preferred), roughly chopped
- 2 tbsp fermented black beans (douchi), rinsed and roughly chopped
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 cups chicken or vegetable stock

*Finishing:*
- 2 tbsp Sichuan peppercorns, toasted in dry wok until fragrant, ground to powder
- 2 tbsp chili oil
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry jasmine rice

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice
Start **5 cups dry jasmine rice**.

### Step 2: Brown the pork (5 min)
Heat **2 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Add **1.5 lbs ground pork**. Break it up and cook until browned and crispy, about 5 minutes. Push to the side of the wok.

### Step 3: Build the base (3 min)
Reduce heat to medium. Add **4 tbsp doubanjiang** to the cleared side of the wok. Stir-fry for 2 minutes until the oil turns red. Add the **garlic**, **ginger**, **scallion whites**, and **fermented black beans**. Stir everything together for 1 minute.

### Step 4: Simmer (10 min)
Pour in **2 cups stock**, **2 tbsp soy sauce**, **1 tbsp Shaoxing wine**, and **1 tsp sugar**. Bring to a simmer. Gently slide in the **blanched tofu cubes**. Do not stir vigorously — swirl the wok gently or push sauce over tofu with the back of a spoon. Simmer 8–10 minutes. The sauce will reduce and thicken naturally.

### Step 5: Finish
Sprinkle in **1.5 tsp ground Sichuan peppercorn**. Drizzle **2 tbsp chili oil** and **1 tsp sesame oil**. Scatter the **scallion greens** on top. One final gentle swirl.

### Step 6: Serve
Ladle over rice. Dust remaining Sichuan peppercorn powder on top.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Day 2 is genuinely the best — the tofu absorbs sauce overnight
- The tofu will break down slightly during storage, which is normal and traditional
- Reheat gently — microwave on medium power or stovetop with a splash of water
- The numbing sensation intensifies over time
