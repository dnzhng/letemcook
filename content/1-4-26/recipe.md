# Korean Gochujang Tofu Rice Bowls
## Week of January 4, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~45 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok

---

## Overview

Crispy tofu glazed in a spicy-sweet gochujang sauce over jasmine rice. Gochujang — Korean fermented red chili paste — is deeply savory with a slow, building heat that's different from any other hot sauce. This is the kind of meal that's even better on day 3 when the tofu has absorbed more sauce.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine rice

**Aromatics**
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- 1-inch knob fresh ginger, grated
- 1 bunch green onions

**Sauce**
- 3–4 tbsp gochujang (adjust to heat preference)
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 2 tbsp neutral oil (for cooking)

**Vegetables (optional)**
- 2 cups broccoli florets, mushrooms, or bell pepper — any quick-cooking veg

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press 20+ min, then cube into ¾-inch pieces.
- **Mix gochujang sauce:** Whisk together gochujang + soy sauce + sesame oil + rice vinegar + sugar in a bowl. Taste — adjust gochujang for heat. Set aside.
- **Mince garlic** and **grate ginger** — combine in one small bowl.
- **Slice green onions** — keep whites and greens separate.
- **Cook rice** per package. Fluff and set aside.
- **Prep any vegetables** (cut into bite-size pieces).

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook rice
Cook rice per package. Fluff and set aside.

### Step 2: Fry tofu (8–10 min)
Heat 2 tbsp neutral oil in wok over medium-high. Add tofu cubes. Fry undisturbed 3–4 min per side until golden and crispy. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Cook aromatics and veg (3–4 min)
In the same wok, add a touch more oil. Add garlic, ginger, and green onion whites. Stir-fry 1 min. Add vegetables and cook 2–3 min until just tender.

### Step 4: Glaze tofu (2–3 min)
Return tofu to wok. Pour gochujang sauce over everything. Toss well over medium-high heat for 2–3 min until sauce thickens and coats.

### Step 5: Finish
Remove from heat. Add green onion greens. Taste and adjust seasoning.

### Step 6: Portion
Serve over rice. Garnish with sesame seeds if desired.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days. Flavor deepens as it sits.
- Reheat with a small splash of water to loosen the sauce.
- Gochujang keeps indefinitely in the fridge — great pantry staple to keep on hand.
