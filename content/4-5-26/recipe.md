# Korean Gochujang Tofu Bowls with Vegetables
## Week of April 5, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~55 minutes
**Total Time:** ~55 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok

---

## Overview

A fully-loaded gochujang tofu stir-fry with broccoli, mushrooms, and bell peppers over jasmine rice. Adapted from Jessica in the Kitchen, The Foodie Takes Flight, Vegan Richa, and Christie at Home. Gochujang's spicy-sweet-fermented depth makes a sauce with no equal. The tofu is NOT coated in cornstarch — only the sauce uses a small amount (~3 tbsp total) for thickening.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine rice

**Produce**
- 4 medium bell peppers (red, yellow, or orange — not green), sliced
- 1.5 lbs cremini or shiitake mushrooms, sliced
- 2 large heads broccoli (~6 cups florets)
- 1 bulb garlic (10 cloves), minced
- 2-inch knob fresh ginger, grated
- 1 bunch green onions

**Gochujang Sauce**
- 1 tub gochujang, 7–8 oz *(look for red rectangular tub — mild or medium heat)*
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 3 tbsp cornstarch dissolved in 4 tbsp cold water *(sauce only, not on tofu)*

**For cooking**
- 2–3 tbsp neutral oil

**Garnish**
- Sesame seeds
- Sliced green onions

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press 20+ min, then cube into ¾-inch pieces. Pat dry.
- **Mix gochujang sauce:** Whisk gochujang + soy sauce + sesame oil + rice vinegar + sugar until smooth. In a separate small bowl, dissolve cornstarch in cold water — combine with sauce just before using. Set aside.
- **Mince garlic** and **grate ginger** — combine in one bowl.
- **Slice all vegetables:** Bell peppers, mushrooms (keep separate from peppers). Cut broccoli into bite-size florets.
- **Slice green onions** — whites and greens separate.
- **Cook rice** per package. Fluff and set aside.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook rice
Cook rice per package. Fluff and set aside.

### Step 2: Fry tofu (8–10 min)
Heat 2 tbsp oil in wok over medium-high. Add tofu cubes. Fry undisturbed 3–4 min per side until golden. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Cook broccoli (4–5 min)
In the same wok over high heat, add broccoli florets with 2 tbsp water. Stir-fry 3–4 min until bright green and just tender. Remove and set aside.

### Step 4: Cook mushrooms and peppers (4 min)
Add a drizzle of oil. Add mushrooms and bell peppers. Stir-fry 3–4 min until mushrooms are golden and peppers are slightly softened.

### Step 5: Add aromatics (1 min)
Add garlic, ginger, and green onion whites. Stir-fry 30–60 seconds.

### Step 6: Add sauce (2–3 min)
Pour gochujang sauce over vegetables. Stir well. Return broccoli and tofu. Toss everything together. Sauce will thicken in 1–2 min from the cornstarch.

### Step 7: Finish and portion
Add green onion greens. Taste and adjust heat with more gochujang or sweetness with more sugar. Serve over rice. Garnish with sesame seeds.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days. Flavor deepens with time.
- Reheat with a splash of water — the sauce thickens in the fridge.
