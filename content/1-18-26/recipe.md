# Thai Peanut Tofu with Rice
## Week of January 18, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~30 minutes
**Total Time:** ~45 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok

---

## Overview

Crispy tofu tossed in a rich, tangy peanut sauce over jasmine rice. The sauce is the star — peanut butter, lime, garlic, ginger, and a hit of chili. Budget Bytes-inspired and adapted for meal prep scale. One of the easiest sauces to make and one of the most satisfying.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine rice

**Aromatics**
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1-inch knob fresh ginger, grated

**Peanut Sauce**
- ½ cup natural peanut butter (no sugar added)
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp lime juice (about 2 limes)
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1–2 tbsp sriracha or sambal oelek (adjust to heat)
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 4–6 tbsp warm water (to thin to pourable consistency)

**For cooking**
- 2 tbsp neutral oil

**Garnish**
- Lime wedges
- Crushed peanuts (optional)
- Fresh cilantro (optional)

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press 20+ min, then cube into ¾-inch pieces.
- **Make peanut sauce:** Whisk peanut butter + soy sauce + lime juice + rice vinegar + sriracha + sesame oil + warm water until smooth and pourable. Taste — adjust lime, soy, or heat. Set aside.
- **Mince garlic** and **grate ginger** — combine in one small bowl.
- **Cook rice** per package. Fluff and set aside.
- **Prep garnishes:** Crush peanuts, pick cilantro, cut lime wedges.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook rice
Cook rice per package. Fluff and set aside.

### Step 2: Fry tofu (8–10 min)
Heat 2 tbsp neutral oil in wok over medium-high. Add tofu cubes. Fry undisturbed 3–4 min per side until golden on all sides. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Fry aromatics (1 min)
In the same wok over medium heat, add garlic and ginger. Stir-fry 30–60 seconds until fragrant.

### Step 4: Add sauce and tofu (2–3 min)
Return tofu to wok. Pour peanut sauce over everything. Toss to coat. Cook over medium heat 2–3 min, stirring, until sauce thickens slightly and clings to the tofu. If sauce gets too thick, add a splash of water.

### Step 5: Portion and garnish
Serve over rice. Garnish with crushed peanuts, cilantro, and a lime wedge.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days refrigerated.
- Peanut sauce thickens significantly in the fridge. Add a splash of water when reheating and stir well.
- Great cold straight from the fridge too — almost like a peanut noodle bowl effect.
