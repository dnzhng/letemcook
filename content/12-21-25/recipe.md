# Mediterranean Lemon-Herb Tofu Bowls
## Week of December 21, 2025

**Servings:** 8 (adjusted that week — normally 10)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~45 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok or large skillet

---

## Overview

Crispy lemon-herb tofu served over rice with a simple tahini-lemon drizzle and fresh herbs. Light and bright — a departure from Asian flavor profiles. The tahini sauce is what makes this: tangy, nutty, and rich enough to tie everything together.

---

## Ingredients (8 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 2.5 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 3 cups dry jasmine or basmati rice

**Aromatics & Produce**
- 6 cloves garlic (3 for tofu, 3 for tahini sauce)
- 3 lemons (juice + zest)
- 1 bunch fresh parsley, roughly chopped
- 1 bunch fresh dill, roughly chopped

**Tofu Marinade**
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Salt and pepper

**Tahini-Lemon Sauce**
- ¼ cup tahini
- Juice of 2 lemons
- 3 cloves garlic, grated
- 4–6 tbsp warm water (to thin)
- Salt to taste

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press 20+ min, then slice into ¾-inch slabs or cubes.
- **Make tahini sauce:** Whisk tahini + lemon juice + grated garlic + warm water until smooth and pourable. Season with salt. Set aside (it will keep all week in the fridge).
- **Zest and juice lemons:** Keep zest separate for tofu marinade; juice goes into tahini sauce.
- **Toss tofu** in olive oil + oregano + garlic powder + lemon zest + salt/pepper. Let sit while rice cooks.
- **Cook rice** per package. Set aside.
- **Chop herbs** and keep refrigerated until serving.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook rice
Cook 3 cups dry rice per package. Fluff and set aside.

### Step 2: Sear tofu (10–12 min)
Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in wok or large skillet over medium-high. Add marinated tofu in a single layer. Sear 4–5 min per side until golden and slightly crisp. Work in batches if needed. Remove.

### Step 3: Assemble
Portion rice into containers. Top with tofu. Drizzle tahini sauce generously. Scatter fresh parsley and dill over the top.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Keep tahini sauce in a separate container; drizzle fresh when eating.
- Sauce thickens in the fridge — stir in a splash of warm water to loosen.
- Stores up to 5 days.

---

## Substitutions

- **Tahini:** Swap for hummus if you don't have tahini.
- **Dill/parsley:** Any fresh herb works — mint, cilantro, or basil are good alternatives.
