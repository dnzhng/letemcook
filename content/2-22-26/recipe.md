# Indonesian Nasi Goreng with Crispy Tofu
## Week of February 22, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~45 minutes (plus rice prep the day before)
**Equipment:** Single wok

---

## Overview

Nasi goreng — Indonesia's national fried rice — gets its distinctive flavor from kecap manis (sweet soy sauce), which creates a caramelized, sweet-savory glaze unlike Chinese or Thai fried rice. Make the rice 1–2 days ahead and refrigerate uncovered: dry, cold rice is the secret to proper fried rice. Adapted from Feasting at Home.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 2 blocks extra-firm tofu (16 oz each, 32 oz total)
- 4 cups dry jasmine or basmati rice (cooked 1–2 days ahead, uncovered in fridge)

**Produce**
- 7 shallots, thinly sliced (or 2 medium yellow onions)
- 13 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 medium red bell peppers, chopped
- 1.5–2 lbs mushrooms (any variety), sliced
- 20 oz frozen peas (or 3⅓ cups fresh shucked)
- 2–3 bunches green onions/scallions, chopped

**Sauce**
- ½ cup + 2 tbsp soy sauce (about 10 tbsp)
- ⅓ cup kecap manis (sweet soy sauce) — or substitute: ¼ cup soy sauce + 3 tbsp brown sugar + 1 tbsp molasses
- 2–3 tbsp sambal oelek (Indonesian chili paste)
- 2 tbsp neutral oil

---

## Mise en Place

- **Cook rice 1–2 days ahead:** Cook rice, spread on a sheet tray, let cool completely, then refrigerate uncovered. Cold dry rice fries; fresh rice steams.
- **Press and cube tofu:** Press 20+ min, cut into ¾-inch cubes.
- **Slice shallots** and **mince garlic** — keep together.
- **Chop bell peppers** and **slice mushrooms** — keep together.
- **Measure sauces** into a small bowl: soy sauce + kecap manis + sambal.
- **Measure frozen peas** — keep frozen until needed.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Fry tofu (8–10 min)
Heat 2 tbsp oil in wok over medium-high. Add tofu cubes. Fry undisturbed 3–4 min per side until golden. Remove and set aside.

### Step 2: Fry shallots and garlic (3–4 min)
In the same wok over high heat, add a drizzle more oil. Add shallots and garlic. Stir-fry 3–4 min until softened and golden.

### Step 3: Add vegetables (3–4 min)
Add bell peppers and mushrooms. Stir-fry over high heat 3–4 min until just cooked through.

### Step 4: Fry rice (5–6 min)
Add cold rice. Toss and press against the wok surface in intervals — let it sit 1 min, toss, repeat. High heat the whole time.

### Step 5: Sauce and combine (2 min)
Pour sauce over rice. Toss well to coat evenly. Add peas and stir-fry 1–2 min until heated through. Return tofu and green onions. Final toss.

### Step 6: Portion
Taste and adjust seasoning with soy sauce or sambal.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days. Gets even better as it sits.
- Reheat in microwave or back in the wok with a splash of water.
- **Kecap manis substitute:** ¼ cup soy sauce + 3 tbsp brown sugar + 1 tbsp molasses, simmered 2 min until slightly thick.
