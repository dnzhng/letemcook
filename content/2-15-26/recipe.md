# Vietnamese Caramelized Tofu (Đậu Hũ Kho Tiêu)
## Week of February 15, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~40 minutes
**Total Time:** ~55 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok

---

## Overview

Kho is a Vietnamese braising technique that creates a sticky, deeply flavored sauce through caramelization rather than cornstarch. Firm tofu (not extra-firm — it absorbs the braising liquid better) and mushrooms braise in a sweet-salty caramel of sugar, fish sauce or soy, and black pepper. Adapted from Full of Plants. Flavors improve dramatically on day 2 and 3.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 3 blocks **firm tofu** (14 oz each) — NOT extra-firm; firm absorbs sauce better
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine rice

**Produce**
- 3 medium shallots, thinly sliced
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 10 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
- 1 bunch green onions

**Caramel & Sauce**
- 5 tbsp granulated white sugar *(for caramel — do not substitute)*
- 4 tbsp vegan fish sauce or soy sauce
- 2 tsp ground black pepper *(go generous — it's in the name)*
- 1 cup water or vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp neutral oil

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu lightly:** Just 10 min — you want it to stay a bit soft to absorb the sauce. Cut into thick rectangles or 1-inch cubes.
- **Slice shallots** thinly and set aside.
- **Mince garlic** and set aside.
- **Slice mushrooms** and set aside.
- **Slice green onions** — keep whites and greens separate.
- **Measure sugar separately** — it goes into the wok alone first.
- **Measure fish sauce/soy and pepper** — combine in a small bowl.
- **Cook rice** per package. Fluff and set aside.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook rice
Cook rice per package. Fluff and set aside.

### Step 2: Fry tofu (8–10 min)
Heat 2 tbsp oil in wok over medium-high. Add tofu. Fry undisturbed 3–4 min per side until golden. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Make caramel (3–5 min) ⚠️ Watch carefully
This is the key step. Wipe wok clean. Add 5 tbsp sugar to the dry wok over medium heat. Do NOT stir — just swirl the pan occasionally. The sugar will melt and turn amber (caramel color) in 3–5 min. The moment it's deep amber and smells nutty, move fast.

### Step 4: Add aromatics (1 min)
Immediately add shallots and garlic to the caramel. Stir quickly — it will bubble aggressively. Stir-fry 45–60 seconds.

### Step 5: Braise (15–20 min)
Add fish sauce/soy + black pepper + water/stock. Stir to dissolve any caramel stuck to the wok. Add mushrooms and return tofu. Bring to a simmer. Cook uncovered 15–20 min, gently swirling occasionally, until sauce reduces by half and becomes thick and glossy.

### Step 6: Finish
Add green onion greens. Taste — adjust pepper and fish sauce. Remove from heat.

### Step 7: Portion
Serve over rice.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days. **This dish tastes significantly better on day 2–3** as the tofu absorbs the sauce overnight.
- Reheat gently — add a splash of water if sauce has thickened too much.
