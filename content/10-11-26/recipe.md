# Sri Lankan Black Curry Tofu
## Week of October 11, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~30 minutes
**Total Time:** ~55 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

"Black" curry gets its name from toasted spices — coriander, cumin, fennel, and fenugreek dry-roasted until dark, giving the curry a deep, smoky, almost coffee-like complexity. Coconut milk rounds it out. Completely different from Indian or Thai curries. Your first Sri Lankan dish.

**Ingredient chain:** Uses leftover onions, canned tomatoes, and spices from last week's Chana Masala. Onions and tomatoes carry to next week's Mexican Tinga.

---

## Mise en Place

*Toasted spice blend (toast in dry wok until dark and fragrant, then grind):*
- 3 tbsp coriander seeds
- 2 tbsp cumin seeds
- 1 tbsp fennel seeds
- 1 tsp fenugreek seeds
- 1 tsp black peppercorns
- 4 cardamom pods
- 2-inch cinnamon stick
- 4 cloves

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into 1-inch cubes

*Curry base:*
- 2 large yellow onions (from last week), sliced
- 8 cloves garlic, sliced
- 2-inch piece ginger (from last week), sliced
- 2 stalks lemongrass (bottom 4 inches), bruised
- 10–15 fresh or dried curry leaves (if available — worth finding)
- 2 cans (14 oz each) diced tomatoes (from last week's buy)
- 2 cans (13.5 oz each) coconut milk
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp turmeric
- Salt to taste

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry basmati or jasmine rice

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Toast the spices (5 min)
In a dry wok over medium heat, add the **coriander, cumin, fennel, fenugreek, peppercorns, cardamom, cinnamon, and cloves**. Toast, stirring constantly, for 3–5 minutes until very dark (darker than you'd normally go) and deeply fragrant. Transfer to a spice grinder or mortar and pestle. Grind to a powder. Set aside.

### Step 2: Cook rice
Start **5 cups dry rice**.

### Step 3: Sear the tofu (8 min)
Heat **3 tbsp coconut oil or neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Fry the **tofu cubes** in two batches until golden, about 4 minutes per batch. Remove to a plate.

### Step 4: Build the curry (5 min)
Add **1 tbsp oil** to the wok. Reduce heat to medium. Add the **onions**, **curry leaves**, and **lemongrass**. Cook 5 minutes until onions are soft. Add the **garlic** and **ginger**. Cook 1 minute.

### Step 5: Add the toasted spice blend and liquids (20 min)
Add the **ground toasted spice blend** and **turmeric**. Stir 1 minute. Add the **diced tomatoes**, **coconut milk**, and **soy sauce**. Bring to a simmer. Return the **tofu** to the wok. Simmer 15–20 minutes until the sauce is rich, dark, and slightly thickened. Season with **salt**.

### Step 6: Serve
Remove the lemongrass stalks. Ladle over rice.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- The toasted spice flavors deepen dramatically overnight — this is a day-2 champion
- The dark color is intentional and beautiful — don't worry if it looks almost black
- Coconut milk keeps the tofu moist during reheating
