# Chinese Black Pepper Tofu
## Week of February 1, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~45 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok

---

## Overview

A Chinese-restaurant classic made at home. The sauce is deeply savory — Shaoxing wine, oyster sauce, dark soy — with a heavy hand of coarsely ground black pepper that gives it a warming, almost spicy edge. This was made before the cornstarch restriction was added; the recipe uses ~7 tbsp total for tofu coating and sauce thickening. If you want to avoid cornstarch, see the substitution note.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine rice

**Aromatics**
- 10 cloves garlic, minced
- 2-inch knob fresh ginger, minced
- 1 bunch green onions, sliced

**Sauce**
- 4 tbsp vegetarian oyster sauce
- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 3 tbsp Shaoxing wine (or dry sherry)
- 2 tbsp coarsely ground black pepper *(key — use more than feels right)*
- 1 tsp sugar
- ½ cup water or vegetable stock
- 3 tbsp cornstarch (for sauce thickening)

**Tofu Coating**
- 4 tbsp cornstarch
- 2 tbsp neutral oil

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press 20+ min, then cut into 1-inch cubes. Pat dry thoroughly.
- **Coat tofu:** Toss cubed tofu in 4 tbsp cornstarch until lightly coated. Shake off excess.
- **Mince garlic and ginger** — combine in one bowl.
- **Mix sauce:** Whisk together oyster sauce + dark soy + light soy + Shaoxing wine + black pepper + sugar + water + 3 tbsp cornstarch. Set aside.
- **Slice green onions** and set aside.
- **Cook rice** per package. Fluff and set aside.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook rice
Cook rice per package. Fluff and set aside.

### Step 2: Fry tofu (10–12 min)
Heat 2 tbsp oil in wok over high heat. Add cornstarch-coated tofu. Fry undisturbed 3–4 min, flip, and fry another 3–4 min. Work in batches to avoid crowding. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Fry aromatics (1 min)
In the same wok, add a drizzle of oil. Add garlic and ginger. Stir-fry 30–60 seconds until fragrant.

### Step 4: Add sauce (3–4 min)
Pour sauce mixture into wok. Stir constantly as it thickens. Once bubbling and thick, return tofu and toss to coat.

### Step 5: Finish
Add green onion whites and toss. Remove from heat. Top with green onion greens.

### Step 6: Portion
Serve over rice.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days refrigerated.
- Sauce firms up significantly when cold — add a splash of water when reheating.

## Cornstarch-Free Option

- For tofu: skip the coating; pat tofu very dry and fry at higher heat for a longer time.
- For sauce: thicken by reducing the sauce longer, or add 1 tbsp rice flour dissolved in 2 tbsp cold water as a substitute.
