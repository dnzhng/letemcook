# Shawarma-Spiced Tofu with Turmeric Rice & Quick Pickled Onions

## Week of June 28, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~65 minutes (includes 30 min tofu press)
**Equipment:** Single large wok with lid

-----

## Overview

Crispy pan-seared tofu coated in a warm, aromatic shawarma spice blend (cumin, coriander, smoked paprika, cinnamon, turmeric) served over golden turmeric-garlic rice with quick pickled red onions and a simple tahini drizzle. Your first Middle Eastern dish in the rotation — earthy, warm, and completely different from the Thai/Indonesian/Jamaican run you've been on. No cornstarch needed; the spice-oil coating crisps naturally in the wok.

**Source:** Adapted from By The Forkful's Tofu Shawarma, Live Eat Learn's Vegan Shawarma, and The Mediterranean Dish's Turmeric Rice.

-----

## Mise en Place (Do This First — ~15 min)

Complete this setup before you turn on the stove. Everything goes into labeled bowls or containers.

### Tofu

- Press **3 blocks extra-firm tofu** (~2.75 lbs total): wrap in clean towels, place a heavy pan or books on top. Press 30 minutes minimum.
- After pressing, cut into ¾-inch cubes.

### Shawarma Spice Blend (mix in a small bowl)

- 1 tbsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp ground coriander
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- ½ tsp ground allspice
- ½ tsp cayenne pepper (adjust to heat preference)
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1½ tsp salt

### Shawarma Coating (mix in a large bowl)

- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Juice of 2 lemons (~4 tbsp)
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- The shawarma spice blend from above
- Whisk together into a paste. Add pressed, cubed tofu and toss to coat evenly.

### Quick Pickled Red Onions (jar or bowl)

- 2 medium red onions, thinly sliced into half-moons
- ½ cup rice vinegar (or white vinegar)
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- Pour hot water over onions, let sit 1 minute, drain. Then toss with vinegar, sugar, and salt. Refrigerate — they'll be ready by the time you're done cooking.

### Turmeric Rice Prep

- Rinse **5 cups dry basmati or jasmine rice** until water runs clear. Drain.
- Dice **1 medium yellow onion** finely.
- Mince **6 cloves garlic.**

### Tahini Sauce (whisk in a small bowl)

- ½ cup tahini
- ¼ cup lemon juice (~2 lemons)
- ¼ cup water (add more for thinner consistency)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced or grated
- ½ tsp salt
- Whisk until smooth and drizzleable. If too thick, add water 1 tbsp at a time.

### Mise en Place Checklist

After prep, you should have:

- [ ] Tofu cubes coated in shawarma spice paste
- [ ] Quick pickled red onions in the fridge
- [ ] Rinsed rice, diced onion, minced garlic ready
- [ ] Tahini sauce whisked and set aside
- [ ] Fresh parsley or cilantro washed (if using, for garnish)

-----

## Cooking Instructions

### Step 1: Cook the turmeric rice in the wok (25 min)

Heat **2 tbsp olive oil** in your wok over medium heat. Add the **diced onion** and sauté 3–4 minutes until softened. Add the **6 cloves minced garlic** and **1½ tsp ground turmeric**, stir for 30 seconds until fragrant.

Add the **5 cups rinsed rice** and stir to coat every grain in the turmeric-onion mixture. Toast for 1–2 minutes.

Pour in **7.5 cups water or vegetable broth** and **1½ tsp salt**. Stir once, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to the lowest setting. Cover with a tight lid (or foil + lid). Cook 15 minutes undisturbed.

Remove from heat, keep covered, and let rest 10 minutes. Fluff with a fork, then transfer rice to a large container or sheet pan. Set aside.

### Step 2: Sear the shawarma tofu in the same wok (12–15 min)

Wipe the wok clean. Heat **3 tbsp olive oil** over medium-high heat until shimmering.

Add the **spice-coated tofu cubes** in a single layer — work in 2–3 batches so they crisp instead of steam. Sear each batch 3–4 minutes per side until deeply golden and crispy on at least two sides. Don't move them too early; let the crust form.

As each batch finishes, transfer to a plate. When all tofu is seared, return everything to the wok, toss gently for 30 seconds to warm through, and kill the heat.

### Step 3: Assemble

Portion **turmeric rice** into containers. Top with **shawarma tofu**. Add a generous pile of **quick pickled red onions** and drizzle with **tahini sauce**. Garnish with **fresh parsley or cilantro** if desired.

-----

## Meal Prep & Storage

- **Fridge:** Up to 5 days in airtight containers.
- **Reheat:** Microwave 2–3 minutes. The tahini thickens as it sits — loosen with a splash of water if needed.
- **Storage tip:** Keep tahini sauce in a separate small container if possible. It reheats fine mixed in, but stays prettiest stored apart.
- **Pickled onions:** Last 2+ weeks in the fridge — make extra if you want them for other meals.
