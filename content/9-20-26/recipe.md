# Vietnamese Bún Thịt Nướng–Style Tofu Noodles
## Week of September 20, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~60 minutes (includes 20 min marination)
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

Cold rice vermicelli topped with lemongrass-marinated grilled/seared tofu, fresh herbs (mint, cilantro, Thai basil), pickled daikon, crushed peanuts, and nước chấm dipping sauce. You've done caramelized tofu and lemongrass tofu, but never this cold noodle format — perfect for early fall warmth.

**Ingredient chain:** Uses leftover limes from last week's Sisig. Limes, peanuts, and fish sauce carry to next week's Burmese Tofu Thoke.

---

## Mise en Place

*Tofu marinade (combine and add tofu):*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, sliced into 1/2-inch steaks
- 4 stalks lemongrass (bottom 4 inches), minced
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tbsp fish sauce
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- Marinate at least 20 min (or overnight)

*Nước chấm sauce (whisk together):*
- 4 tbsp fish sauce
- 4 tbsp lime juice (from last week's limes)
- 4 tbsp sugar
- 4 tbsp warm water
- 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 2 Thai chilies, sliced thin

*Quick-pickled daikon (combine and let sit 15 min):*
- 1 medium daikon radish, julienned
- 1/3 cup rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt

*Noodles:*
- 1.5 lbs dried rice vermicelli

*Toppings (prep into bowls):*
- 1 bunch fresh mint
- 1 bunch fresh cilantro
- 1 bunch Thai basil (if available)
- 1 cup crushed roasted peanuts
- 1 head butter lettuce, shredded

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook noodles
Soak or boil **1.5 lbs rice vermicelli** per package directions. Drain, rinse with cold water. Toss with **1 tsp oil** to prevent sticking.

### Step 2: Sear the tofu (10 min)
Heat **3 tbsp oil** in the wok over high heat. Sear the **marinated tofu steaks** for 3–4 minutes per side until deeply charred and caramelized. Work in batches. Remove and slice into strips.

### Step 3: Assemble
Divide **cold noodles** among containers. Top with **shredded lettuce**, **tofu strips**, **pickled daikon**, **herbs**, and **crushed peanuts**. Pack **nước chấm** in small separate containers.

### Step 4: Serve
Pour nước chấm over the bowl and toss everything together before eating.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Keep sauce separate until serving — this is critical for keeping noodles from getting soggy
- The fresh herbs wilt after day 3 — add fresh herbs mid-week if possible
- This bowl is best eaten cold or at room temperature, so no reheating needed
