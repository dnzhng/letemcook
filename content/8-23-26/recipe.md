# Peruvian Lomo Saltado–Style Tofu
## Week of August 23, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~30 minutes
**Total Time:** ~50 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

A Chinese-Peruvian fusion stir-fry — soy sauce, vinegar, tomatoes, and red onion tossed in a screaming-hot wok. Lomo Saltado is traditionally made with beef, but tofu takes on the tangy, smoky sauce beautifully. This dish traces back to 19th-century Chinese immigrants in Peru (chifa cuisine). Serve over rice. Your first Peruvian dish.

**Ingredient chain:** Uses leftover tomatoes and onion from last week's Menemen. Onions and potatoes carry to next week's Japanese Curry.

---

## Mise en Place

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into thick batons (like steak fries)

*Vegetables (cut and set separately):*
- 3 large red onions, cut into thick wedges (petals)
- 6 Roma tomatoes, cut into wedges
- 2 ají amarillo peppers, seeded and sliced (or 2 tbsp ají amarillo paste, or sub 1 yellow bell pepper + 1 tsp cayenne)
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 bunch fresh cilantro, chopped

*Sauce (whisk together):*
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp red wine vinegar (or white vinegar)
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp salt

*Optional but traditional:*
- 2 lbs russet potatoes, cut into thick fries and oven-baked at 425°F for 25 min while you cook

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry jasmine rice

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice and optional fries
Start **5 cups dry jasmine rice**. If making fries: toss **potato batons** in **2 tbsp oil** and **1 tsp salt**, spread on a baking sheet, bake at 425°F for 25 minutes.

### Step 2: Sear the tofu (8 min)
Heat **3 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over the highest heat possible. Sear the **tofu batons** in two batches until deeply browned on 2–3 sides, about 4 minutes per batch. Remove to a plate.

### Step 3: Char the vegetables (4 min)
Keep the wok screaming hot. Add **1 tbsp oil**. Add the **red onion wedges** — let them char without stirring for 1–2 minutes. Add the **tomato wedges** and **ají amarillo**. Toss for 2 minutes. The tomatoes should be softened but still holding their shape.

### Step 4: Sauce and finish (2 min)
Add the **garlic** and stir 30 seconds. Pour in the **sauce mixture**. Return the **tofu**. Toss everything together over high heat for 1–2 minutes until the sauce reduces and coats everything.

### Step 5: Serve
Portion rice into containers. Add the stir-fry. Toss the **baked fries** on top (or on the side). Garnish with **cilantro**.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- The tomatoes will break down slightly overnight — this is fine, they become part of the sauce
- Fries are best made fresh, but you can store them separately and reheat in a toaster oven
- The vinegar kick keeps this tasting bright even after a few days
