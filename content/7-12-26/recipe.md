# Thai Green Curry Tofu
## Week of July 12, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~25 minutes
**Total Time:** ~45 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

Bright, herbal, and coconut-rich — Thai green curry is lighter and more aromatic than the red curry you've done before. Green chilies, basil, and makrut lime leaf give it a fresh, almost grassy quality. Bamboo shoots add crunch. You've done red curry, peanut, pad krapow, pad see ew, and drunken noodles — this fills the green curry gap.

**Ingredient chain:** Uses leftover coconut milk and limes from last week's laksa. Shares nothing unique with next week, so clean break.

---

## Mise en Place

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into 1-inch cubes

*Vegetables (slice and set aside together):*
- 3 bell peppers (any color except green), sliced into strips
- 1 can (14 oz) bamboo shoots, drained and sliced
- 2 small zucchini, halved lengthwise and sliced into half-moons
- 1 large bunch Thai basil (or Italian basil), leaves picked

*Sauce ingredients (measure and set together):*
- 5 tbsp Thai green curry paste (Mae Ploy or Maesri brand)
- 2 cans (13.5 oz each) coconut milk — leftover from laksa week
- 2 cups vegetable stock or water
- 3 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp palm sugar or brown sugar
- 3 limes — leftover from laksa week
- 4–6 makrut lime leaves (optional, but excellent)

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry jasmine rice

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice
Start **5 cups dry jasmine rice** in a rice cooker or pot. Set aside when done.

### Step 2: Sear the tofu (8 min)
Heat **3 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Fry the **tofu cubes** in two batches until golden, about 4 minutes per batch. Remove to a plate.

### Step 3: Bloom the curry paste (2 min)
Reduce heat to medium. Spoon the thick cream from the top of **1 can coconut milk** into the wok. Add **5 tbsp green curry paste**. Stir-fry for 2 minutes until fragrant and the paste darkens slightly.

### Step 4: Build the curry (10 min)
Pour in the rest of **both cans coconut milk** and **2 cups stock**. Add **3 tbsp fish sauce**, **1 tbsp sugar**, and **makrut lime leaves** (if using). Bring to a simmer. Add the **bell peppers**, **bamboo shoots**, and **zucchini**. Simmer 8–10 minutes until vegetables are just tender.

### Step 5: Finish
Return the **tofu** to the wok. Stir gently. Squeeze in the juice of **2 limes**. Tear in the **Thai basil leaves** and stir until just wilted. Taste — add more fish sauce for salt or more sugar if needed.

### Step 6: Serve
Portion rice into containers. Ladle curry over rice. Garnish with remaining lime wedges.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Green curry reheats beautifully — the coconut milk keeps everything moist
- The basil will darken after a day but the flavor remains excellent
- Day 2–3 portions are the best as flavors develop
- Store rice separately if you prefer non-soggy rice
