# Burmese Chickpea Tofu Thoke (Salad)
## Week of September 27, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~30 minutes
**Total Time:** ~4 hours (chickpea tofu needs to set in fridge — make day before)
**Equipment:** Single large wok + a sheet pan

---

## Overview

Not soy tofu — Burmese "tofu" (shan tofu) is made from chickpea flour and water, set into sliceable blocks like polenta. Toss with lime, fish sauce, chili flakes, cilantro, crispy fried onions, and roasted peanuts. Completely unique texture and flavor. Make the chickpea tofu a day ahead (it sets in the fridge).

**Ingredient chain:** Uses leftover limes, peanuts, and fish sauce from last week's Bún Noodles. Chickpea flour theme connects to next week's Chana Masala.

---

## Mise en Place (Day Before)

*Chickpea tofu (make ahead — needs 3+ hours to set):*
- 3 cups chickpea flour (besan)
- 6 cups water
- 1.5 tsp salt

*Day-of prep:*

*Dressing (whisk together):*
- 4 tbsp fish sauce (from last week)
- 4 tbsp lime juice (from last week's limes)
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 2 tsp chili flakes
- 4 cloves garlic, finely minced

*Toppings:*
- 1 cup roasted peanuts (from last week), roughly crushed
- 4 shallots, thinly sliced and fried crispy (or use store-bought fried onions)
- 1 bunch fresh cilantro, chopped
- 2 tbsp toasted chickpea flour (toast in dry wok for 2 min)

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry jasmine rice

---

## Instructions

### Day Before: Make the chickpea tofu
Whisk **3 cups chickpea flour** with **3 cups cold water** until smooth (no lumps). In the wok, bring **3 cups water** and **1.5 tsp salt** to a boil. Reduce to medium-low. Pour in the chickpea slurry while stirring constantly. Stir vigorously for 8–10 minutes until the mixture thickens into a very thick paste that pulls away from the sides. Pour into an oiled sheet pan (or 9×13 baking dish). Smooth the top. Cool to room temp, then refrigerate uncovered for at least 3 hours (overnight is best).

### Day Of:

### Step 1: Cook rice
Start **5 cups dry jasmine rice**.

### Step 2: Fry the shallots (5 min)
Heat **1/2 cup neutral oil** in the wok over medium heat. Add the **sliced shallots**. Fry, stirring often, until deep golden brown (5–7 minutes). Remove with a slotted spoon to paper towels. Reserve the shallot oil.

### Step 3: Cut and sear the chickpea tofu (8 min)
Cut the set chickpea tofu into 1-inch cubes or thick slices. Heat **2 tbsp shallot oil** in the wok over high heat. Sear the **tofu pieces** until golden and crispy on 2–3 sides, about 3–4 minutes per batch. You want a crispy exterior with a creamy interior.

### Step 4: Assemble the thoke
In a large bowl, gently toss the **seared chickpea tofu** with the **dressing**. Add the **crushed peanuts**, **fried shallots** (save some for topping), and **cilantro**. Toss gently. Dust with **toasted chickpea flour**.

### Step 5: Serve
Portion rice into containers. Top with the thoke. Garnish with remaining fried shallots.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- The chickpea tofu firms up more each day — day 1 is creamier, day 3–4 is firmer (both are good)
- Store dressing separately if you want crisper tofu — toss before eating
- Fried shallots lose their crunch after day 1 in the fridge — consider making a small fresh batch mid-week
