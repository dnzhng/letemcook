# Filipino Adobo Tofu (Adobong Tokwa) with Wok-Wilted Bok Choy
## Week of April 12, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~45 minutes
**Total Time:** ~55 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok

---

## Overview

Filipino adobo is the ultimate meal-prep dish — a braise of vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, bay leaves, and peppercorns that tastes even better as leftovers because the tofu keeps absorbing the sauce. Key technique: do NOT stir the vinegar for the first 5 minutes of simmering — traditional Filipino method that mellows acidity and prevents harshness. 20 cloves of garlic sounds excessive; it is not. Adapted from The Foodie Takes Flight, Plant-Based on a Budget, and Health My Lifestyle.

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine rice

**Produce**
- 2 bulbs garlic (~20 cloves), smashed and peeled *(go heavy — this is traditional)*
- 3 medium yellow onions, sliced into half-moons (or substitute shallots)
- 1 bunch green onions/scallions
- 1.5 lbs baby bok choy (~6–8 heads), halved

**Adobo Sauce**
- 1 cup soy sauce
- ¾ cup white vinegar or cane vinegar *(NOT rice vinegar — use plain white or Filipino cane vinegar)*
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1.5 tbsp whole black peppercorns
- 6–8 bay leaves
- 1 cup water

**For cooking**
- 2 tbsp neutral oil

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press 20+ min, then cut into 1-inch cubes.
- **Smash and peel garlic** — all 20 cloves. Don't mince; keep whole or halved.
- **Slice onions** into half-moons and set aside.
- **Halve bok choy** lengthwise. Set aside.
- **Measure soy sauce, vinegar, brown sugar** — combine in a pitcher or bowl. Add bay leaves, peppercorns, and water. This is your adobo braising liquid.
- **Slice green onions** for garnish.
- **Cook rice** per package. Fluff and set aside.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook rice
Cook rice per package. Fluff and set aside.

### Step 2: Fry tofu (8–10 min)
Heat 2 tbsp oil in wok over medium-high. Add tofu cubes. Fry 3–4 min per side until golden. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Sauté onions and garlic (4 min)
In the same wok over medium heat, add onions and garlic. Sauté 3–4 min until softened and golden.

### Step 4: Add braising liquid — DO NOT STIR for first 5 min
Pour adobo braising liquid over onions and garlic. Bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce to a simmer. **Do not stir for the first 5 minutes.** This allows the vinegar to mellow. After 5 min, stir and add tofu.

### Step 5: Braise tofu (20–25 min)
Add fried tofu to the wok. Simmer uncovered 20–25 min, turning tofu occasionally, until sauce reduces by about half and becomes glossy. The tofu will darken and absorb the adobo.

### Step 6: Wilt bok choy (3 min)
Push tofu to the side. Add bok choy cut-side down. Cook 2–3 min until just wilted and bright green.

### Step 7: Finish and portion
Remove bay leaves. Taste and adjust with more soy sauce or a splash more vinegar. Serve tofu and bok choy over rice. Garnish with green onions.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days. **Day 2–3 is the best** — tofu absorbs more sauce overnight.
- Reheat gently with a splash of water.
- Garlic cloves become sweet and tender; they're meant to be eaten.
