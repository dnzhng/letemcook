# Sesame Ginger Tofu with Noodles
## Week of December 14, 2025

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~45 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok + pot for noodles

---

## Overview

Crispy pan-fried tofu tossed with noodles in a savory sesame-ginger sauce. Adapted from Little Spice Jar — sugar reduced from the original based on reviewer feedback (many found it too sweet). Works with lo mein, udon, or soba; lo mein holds up best for meal prep.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 1 lb lo mein noodles (or udon or soba)

**Aromatics**
- 2-inch knob fresh ginger, grated
- 6 cloves garlic, minced

**Sauce**
- 6 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp sesame oil (toasted)
- 3 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tsp sugar (reduced from original)
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (for cooking)

**Garnish**
- 1 bunch green onions, sliced
- 2 tbsp sesame seeds

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press at least 20 min, then cube into ¾-inch pieces.
- **Grate ginger** and **mince garlic** — combine in one small bowl.
- **Mix sauce:** Whisk together 6 tbsp soy sauce + 3 tbsp sesame oil + 3 tbsp rice vinegar + 2 tsp sugar. Set aside.
- **Slice green onions** and set aside.
- **Boil water** for noodles (cook just before or during tofu step).

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook noodles
Cook noodles per package until just al dente. Drain, rinse with cold water to stop cooking, toss with a drizzle of sesame oil to prevent sticking. Set aside.

### Step 2: Fry tofu (8–10 min)
Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in wok over medium-high. Add tofu cubes. Fry undisturbed 3–4 min per side until golden. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Fry aromatics (1 min)
In the same wok over medium heat, add ginger and garlic. Stir constantly for 45–60 seconds until fragrant but not browned.

### Step 4: Add noodles and sauce (3 min)
Add noodles to wok. Pour sauce over everything. Toss with tongs over medium heat until noodles are evenly coated and sauce is absorbed, about 2–3 min.

### Step 5: Combine and finish
Return tofu to wok. Toss gently to combine without breaking up tofu. Remove from heat.

### Step 6: Garnish
Top with green onions and sesame seeds.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days refrigerated.
- Noodles absorb sauce as they sit — add a small splash of water or soy sauce when reheating.
- Reheat in microwave or briefly in the wok.
