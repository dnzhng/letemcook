# Sichuan Dry-Fried Broccoli with Crispy Tofu
## Week of February 8, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~45 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok

---

## Overview

Adapted from The Woks of Life's Sichuan dry-fried green beans — swapped to broccoli for Trader Joe's friendliness. The dry-searing technique (no oil, high heat) removes moisture from the broccoli and creates charred, blistered edges with concentrated flavor. No cornstarch. The wok does all the work.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine rice

**Produce**
- 2 large heads broccoli (~6 cups florets)
- 10 cloves garlic, minced
- 2-inch knob fresh ginger, minced
- 6–8 dried red chilies (árbol or Chinese dried chilies)

**Sauce**
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp sugar
- Neutral oil — for frying tofu

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press 20+ min, then cut into ¾-inch cubes. Pat very dry.
- **Cut broccoli** into bite-size florets. Make sure they are completely dry — pat with paper towels if washed. Moisture is the enemy of dry-frying.
- **Mince garlic and ginger** — combine in one bowl.
- **Prep chilies:** Break dried chilies in half; shake out seeds if you want less heat.
- **Mix sauce:** Stir together soy sauce + sesame oil + sugar. Set aside.
- **Cook rice** per package. Fluff and set aside.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook rice
Cook rice per package. Fluff and set aside.

### Step 2: Fry tofu (8–10 min)
Heat 2 tbsp oil in wok over medium-high. Add tofu cubes. Fry undisturbed 3–4 min per side until golden. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Dry-sear broccoli (12–15 min)
This is the key step. Wipe wok clean. Heat wok over HIGH heat until very hot — no oil. Add broccoli florets in a single layer (work in batches). Press against the wok with a spatula and let sit undisturbed 2–3 min. You want char marks and blistering. Toss, press again, repeat. Total 12–15 min. The broccoli should be tender-ish with deeply blistered edges. Remove and set aside.

### Step 4: Fry aromatics (1 min)
Add 1 tbsp oil to wok. Add dried chilies — fry 30 seconds. Add garlic and ginger — fry 30 more seconds.

### Step 5: Combine and sauce (2 min)
Return broccoli and tofu to wok. Pour sauce over. Toss well over high heat 1–2 min.

### Step 6: Portion
Serve over rice.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days. Broccoli softens a little more as it sits but stays flavorful.
- Reheat in microwave or briefly back in the wok over high heat to re-crisp.
