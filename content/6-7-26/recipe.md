# Jamaican Jerk Tofu with Coconut Rice & Peas
## Week of June 7, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~40 minutes
**Total Time:** ~80 minutes (includes 30 min hands-off tofu press)
**Equipment:** Single large wok with lid (cook rice & peas first, set aside, then sear tofu in the same wok)

---

## Overview

A bright, bold island reset after a spring of stews and stir-fries. Crispy seared tofu gets tossed in a smoky-sweet-spicy jerk glaze, served over creamy coconut "rice and peas" (peas = red kidney beans, Caribbean-style) cooked with thyme, scallion, garlic, and allspice. No cornstarch — the tofu crisps from a good sear on well-pressed cubes, and BBQ sauce + wet jerk paste give you all the cling you need. First Caribbean dish in your rotation.

**Note on heat:** Wet jerk seasoning (Walkerswood is the easy-to-find standard) is genuinely hot. Start with less and taste as you go. A whole, un-pierced Scotch bonnet dropped into the rice perfumes it without making it fiery — pull it out before serving.

---

## Ingredients

**Tofu & Protein:**
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each, ~2.75 lbs total), pressed
- 3 tbsp neutral oil (for searing — vegetable, canola, or avocado)

**Jerk glaze:**
- 1 cup vegetable broth (for the quick marinade)
- 1 tbsp BBQ seasoning (dry rub)
- 1 cup BBQ sauce (a plain/basic one — let the jerk be the star)
- 3 tbsp wet jerk seasoning/paste (Walkerswood, or any wet — not dry — jerk; adjust to heat tolerance)

**Coconut Rice & Peas:**
- 4½ cups long-grain white rice (dry) → ~10 cups cooked
- 2 cans (15 oz each) red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk
- ~4 cups water (plus more as needed)
- 1 bunch scallions (6–8 stalks), trimmed and bruised/smashed
- 6 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 small bundle fresh thyme (8–10 sprigs) or 1 tbsp dried
- 1½ tsp ground allspice (or 1 tbsp whole allspice berries / "pimento")
- 1 whole Scotch bonnet pepper, left whole and un-pierced (optional but traditional)
- 2 tsp salt (adjust to taste)

**To finish (optional):**
- Extra scallions, sliced
- Fresh cilantro
- Lime wedges

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Press the Tofu (30 minutes, hands-off)
Press all **3 blocks of tofu** to drive out moisture — the drier the tofu, the better it sears and absorbs the glaze. Wrap each block in a clean towel, set a cutting board on top weighted with something heavy (a cast-iron pan, a few cans), and leave **30 minutes** (overnight in the fridge is even better). After pressing, cut into **1-inch cubes** (cubes crisp more evenly than torn pieces).

> Start the rice & peas during this press window so everything finishes together.

---

### Step 2 — Build the Coconut Rice & Peas (in the wok, ~30 min)
Rinse **4½ cups rice** under cold water until the water runs mostly clear; drain.

In your **wok**, combine the rinsed rice, **2 cans drained kidney beans**, the full **can of coconut milk**, **~4 cups water**, **6 smashed garlic cloves**, the **bruised scallions**, **thyme**, **1½ tsp allspice**, the **whole Scotch bonnet** (if using), and **2 tsp salt**. Stir once.

Bring to a boil over high heat, then **reduce to low, cover, and simmer ~20 minutes** until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is tender. Check at 18 minutes — if it's dry but not quite done, add a splash more water and cover again.

Turn off the heat and let it sit, covered, **5 minutes**. Fluff with a fork. Fish out the scallion stalks, thyme stems, and the whole Scotch bonnet. **Transfer the rice & peas to a large container or bowl and keep warm** — you'll reuse the wok for the tofu.

> Liquid ratio is roughly 1:1.5 rice-to-liquid here because coconut milk + bean starch thickens things. Long-grain (not jasmine) holds its texture best for meal prep.

---

### Step 3 — Quick-Marinate the Tofu (during the rice simmer, ~15 min)
In a large bowl, toss the **tofu cubes** with **1 cup vegetable broth** and **1 tbsp BBQ seasoning**. Let sit **~15 minutes** so the cubes drink up the seasoned broth. Drain off any broth that hasn't absorbed before searing.

---

### Step 4 — Sear the Tofu (in the same wok, ~12–15 min)
Wipe the wok, set it over **medium-high heat**, and add **3 tbsp oil**. When shimmering, add the tofu in a **single layer** — work in 2 batches so it sears instead of steaming. Cook each batch **3–4 minutes per side** until golden and crisp on at least two sides. Don't crowd or fuss with it; let the crust form. Set seared tofu aside between batches.

---

### Step 5 — Glaze (~3 min)
Return all the tofu to the wok over **medium heat**. Add **1 cup BBQ sauce** and **3 tbsp wet jerk seasoning** (start with 2 tbsp if you're heat-sensitive, taste, then add more). Toss gently to coat every cube. Let it sit **2–3 minutes** so the glaze tightens and soaks in. Kill the heat.

---

### Step 6 — Plate & Portion
Spoon coconut rice & peas into each container, top with glazed jerk tofu, and finish with sliced scallions, cilantro, and a lime wedge if you like. For meal prep, the tofu keeps absorbing the glaze overnight — day 2 is even better. Reheat with a splash of water if the rice has dried out.

---

## Make-Ahead / Meal Prep Notes
- Keeps 4–5 days refrigerated in airtight containers.
- Freezes up to ~3 months (glazed tofu freezes well; rice is okay but best fresh).
- Reheat in microwave or low on the stovetop with a splash of water or broth.
