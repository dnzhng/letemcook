# Jamaican Curry Tofu
## Week of November 29, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~25 minutes
**Total Time:** ~55 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

Jamaican curry uses a unique curry powder heavy on allspice, scotch bonnet, and thyme — bright and aromatic in a way Indian curry isn't. You did jerk tofu back in June — this is the other essential Jamaican flavor profile. Potatoes, tofu, onion, and coconut milk. Warm, golden, and deeply comforting.

**Ingredient chain:** Clean start. Coconut milk and potatoes carry to next week's Thai Massaman... wait, Massaman was November 9. Clean break — but coconut milk carries into December's Malaysian Nasi Lemak.

---

## Mise en Place

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into 1-inch cubes

*Vegetables:*
- 3 large yellow onions, diced
- 2 lbs Yukon gold potatoes, peeled and cut into 1-inch chunks
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 scotch bonnet or habanero peppers, seeded and minced (use gloves!)
- 6 sprigs fresh thyme (or 2 tsp dried thyme)

*Curry sauce:*
- 3 tbsp Jamaican curry powder (or 2 tbsp regular curry powder + 1 tsp allspice)
- 2 cans (13.5 oz each) coconut milk
- 2 cups vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp allspice
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- 2 scallions, sliced (garnish)

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry jasmine rice

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice
Start **5 cups dry jasmine rice**.

### Step 2: Sear the tofu (8 min)
Heat **3 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Fry the **tofu cubes** until golden, about 4 minutes per batch. Remove.

### Step 3: Build the curry base (5 min)
Add **1 tbsp oil**. Reduce heat to medium. Add the **onions** and cook 4 minutes. Add the **garlic**, **scotch bonnet**, and **thyme**. Cook 1 minute. Add the **curry powder** and **allspice**. Stir 1 minute until fragrant and toasted.

### Step 4: Simmer (25 min)
Add the **potatoes**, **coconut milk**, **stock**, and **soy sauce**. Bring to a boil, reduce to simmer. Cook 20–25 minutes until potatoes are fork-tender and the sauce has thickened.

### Step 5: Finish
Return the **tofu**. Simmer 3 more minutes. Season with **salt** and **pepper**. Garnish with **scallions**.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- The scotch bonnet heat is front-loaded — it mellows each day as the curry sits
- Potatoes absorb the curry beautifully overnight
- Keep the scotch bonnet whole (dropped in and fished out) if you want aroma without intense heat
