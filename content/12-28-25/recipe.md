# Mexican Chipotle Tofu Burrito Bowls
## Week of December 28, 2025

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~50 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok

---

## Overview

Smoky chipotle-marinated tofu over cilantro-lime rice with black beans and a quick lime-cilantro slaw from leftover purple cabbage. Big, bold Tex-Mex flavor — completely different from the Asian rotation. The chipotle adobo sauce is the backbone of everything.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine rice

**Produce**
- ½ head purple cabbage, thinly shredded (leftover)
- 3 limes
- 1 bunch cilantro
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced

**Pantry**
- 2–3 chipotle peppers in adobo + 2 tbsp adobo sauce (from a can)
- 2 cans black beans (15 oz each), drained and rinsed
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press 20+ min, then cube into 1-inch pieces.
- **Make chipotle marinade:** Blend or finely chop 2–3 chipotle peppers + 2 tbsp adobo sauce + 2 tbsp olive oil + 1 tsp cumin + 1 tsp smoked paprika + salt. Toss tofu in marinade and let sit while you prep everything else.
- **Make lime-cilantro slaw:** Toss shredded cabbage with juice of 1 lime + handful of chopped cilantro + pinch of salt. Set aside in the fridge.
- **Mince garlic** and **dice onion**.
- **Cook rice:** Cook per package. Once done, stir in juice of 1 lime + large handful of chopped cilantro + salt.
- **Drain and rinse black beans**.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook cilantro-lime rice
Cook rice. When done, fluff and stir in lime juice + cilantro + salt. Set aside.

### Step 2: Sear tofu (10–12 min)
Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in wok over medium-high. Add marinated tofu in batches. Sear 3–4 min per side until caramelized and slightly charred on edges. The adobo marinade will darken and stick — that's good. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Cook beans (5 min)
In the same wok over medium heat, add a drizzle of oil. Add garlic and onion. Sauté 3 min. Add black beans + 1 tsp cumin + pinch of salt. Stir and cook 2–3 min until warmed through.

### Step 4: Assemble
Portion rice into containers. Add black beans. Top with tofu. Add a scoop of cabbage slaw on the side.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Store slaw separately if possible — it stays crisp longer.
- Stores up to 5 days refrigerated.
- Add a squeeze of lime when serving to brighten everything up.
