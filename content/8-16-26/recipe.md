# Turkish Menemen-Style Tofu with Rice
## Week of August 16, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~25 minutes
**Total Time:** ~40 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

Scrambled eggs and crumbled tofu braised in a spiced tomato-pepper sauce with cumin, Aleppo pepper (or red pepper flakes), and fresh herbs. A one-wok Turkish breakfast dish turned into a hearty meal-prep dinner. The tofu absorbs the tomato sauce like a sponge and the eggs add richness.

**Ingredient chain:** Uses leftover canned tomatoes, onions, and cumin from last week's Ethiopian Misir Wot. Tomatoes and onion carry to next week's Peruvian Lomo Saltado.

---

## Mise en Place

*Tofu and eggs:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, crumbled by hand into rough chunks
- 8 large eggs

*Vegetables (chop and set together):*
- 2 large yellow onions (from last week), diced
- 4 bell peppers (mix of colors), diced
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cans (14 oz each) diced tomatoes (from last week's buy)
- 2 tbsp tomato paste

*Spices:*
- 2 tsp ground cumin (from last week)
- 2 tsp Aleppo pepper (or 1 tsp red pepper flakes + 1 tsp paprika)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt and black pepper to taste

*Herbs:*
- 1 bunch fresh parsley, chopped
- Fresh dill (optional), chopped

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry jasmine or basmati rice

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice
Start **5 cups dry rice**.

### Step 2: Sear the tofu (6 min)
Heat **3 tbsp olive oil** in the wok over high heat. Add the **crumbled tofu** and cook, stirring occasionally, until golden and crispy on the edges, about 6 minutes. Remove to a plate.

### Step 3: Soften vegetables (5 min)
Add **1 tbsp olive oil** to the wok. Reduce heat to medium. Add the **onions** and cook 3 minutes. Add the **bell peppers** and **garlic**. Cook 2 more minutes.

### Step 4: Build the sauce (10 min)
Add the **cumin**, **Aleppo pepper**, and **smoked paprika**. Stir 30 seconds. Add the **diced tomatoes** and **tomato paste**. Simmer 8–10 minutes until the sauce thickens and the peppers are soft. Season with **1 tsp salt** and **black pepper**.

### Step 5: Add eggs and tofu (3 min)
Return the **tofu** to the wok. Crack the **8 eggs** directly into the sauce, spacing them around the wok. Stir gently — you want some chunks of egg mixed through, not fully scrambled. Cook 2–3 minutes until eggs are just set.

### Step 6: Finish
Scatter **parsley** (and dill if using) over the top. Serve over rice.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- The eggs will continue to cook when reheated — they'll be fully set but still tender
- The tomato sauce keeps everything moist during storage
- Aleppo pepper is milder and fruitier than regular red pepper flakes — worth seeking out
