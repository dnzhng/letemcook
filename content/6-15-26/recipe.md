# Indonesian Tahu Kecap (Sweet Soy Glazed Tofu) 🇮🇩

### Over Jasmine Rice · Single Wok · 10 Servings (2/day × 5 days)

Crispy golden tofu tossed in a glossy, molasses-rich sweet-soy glaze with garlic, shallots, ginger, and chili. Sweet–salty–savory with a gentle kick. No cornstarch — the sweet soy reduces into its own glaze. This version skips store-bought kecap manis and builds the sweet soy from soy sauce + brown sugar. Avoids green beans and carrots, made start-to-finish in one wok.

**Total time:** ~45 min · **Cost:** ~$1.50–2.20/serving

-----

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Tofu & Grain**

- 4 blocks (≈3.5 lb / 1.6 kg total) firm or extra-firm tofu
- 4.5 cups dry jasmine rice

**Aromatics & Produce**

- 12 cloves garlic, minced
- 5 shallots, thinly sliced (or 1 medium onion + 2 shallots)
- 3-inch piece fresh ginger, peeled & julienned
- 2 red bell peppers + 1 green bell pepper, cut into bite-size pieces
- 4–6 red chilies (Fresno or bird's eye), sliced — adjust to heat preference
- 1 bunch scallions, sliced (garnish)
- 2 limes, cut into wedges (garnish)

**Sweet Soy Glaze** (replaces kecap manis)

- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/2 cup brown sugar, packed
- 1 tsp molasses (optional, for depth)
- 2 tbsp brown sugar, extra — add only to taste if needed
- 4 bay leaves (Indonesian/Indian bay if available)
- 2 cups vegetable stock or water
- Black pepper, to taste

**Assumed on hand:** cooking oil, salt, black pepper.

-----

## Instructions

### 1. Cook the rice

Rinse **4.5 cups jasmine rice** until water runs clear. Cook with **~6.75 cups water** (rice cooker or covered pot, 15 min simmer + 10 min rest). Fluff and set aside.

### 2. Mix the sweet soy glaze

In a small bowl, stir **1/2 cup soy sauce**, **1/2 cup packed brown sugar**, and **1 tsp molasses** (if using) until the sugar mostly dissolves. This is your kecap manis stand-in. Set aside.

### 3. Press & cube the tofu

Pat **all 4 blocks (3.5 lb) tofu** dry and press 10–15 min under a weight to remove water. Cut into 1-inch cubes.

### 4. Pan-fry the tofu

Heat **3–4 tbsp oil** in the wok over medium-high. Fry the tofu cubes in batches, turning, until golden and lightly crisp on most sides (~6–8 min per batch). Remove to a plate. Don't crowd the wok.

### 5. Build the aromatic base

In the same wok, add **1 tbsp oil** if dry. Sauté **12 cloves minced garlic**, **5 sliced shallots**, and the **3-inch knob julienned ginger** over medium heat for ~3 min until fragrant and softened.

### 6. Add peppers & chilies

Add the **2 red + 1 green bell pepper** and **4–6 sliced chilies**. Stir-fry 2–3 min until just crisp-tender.

### 7. Simmer the glaze

Pour in the **sweet soy glaze mix**, **4 bay leaves**, and **2 cups stock/water**. Stir and bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer for 5 min until slightly thickened. Taste — if you want it sweeter, stir in up to **2 tbsp extra brown sugar**. Season with **black pepper**.

### 8. Glaze the tofu

Return the fried tofu to the wok. Toss to coat, then simmer uncovered **8–10 min**, stirring occasionally, until the sauce reduces and clings to the tofu in a glossy glaze. Discard bay leaves.

### 9. Finish & serve

Top with **sliced scallions**. Serve over jasmine rice with **lime wedges** to squeeze over.

-----

## Meal Prep & Storage

- Portion into 10 containers (rice + glazed tofu). Keep **lime wedges separate**, squeeze fresh when eating.
- Fridge: up to 5 days. The glaze deepens overnight.
- Reheat: microwave 1.5–2 min, or quick re-stir in a hot pan. Add a splash of water if the glaze tightened.

-----

## Notes & Swaps

- **Sweet soy glaze:** The soy + brown sugar mix is a touch saltier and thinner than bottled kecap manis. The molasses pushes it closer to the real thing; if you ever pick up kecap manis, swap in 3/4 cup for the glaze mix and drop the separate soy sauce.
- **Heat control:** Seeds removed = milder; add a spoon of sambal oelek for more fire.
- **Extra depth (optional):** 1 tsp tamarind concentrate stirred into the sauce adds a tangy edge.
- **No bay leaves?** Fine to skip; they're aromatic background only.
