# Japanese Miso Glazed Eggplant & Tofu Rice Bowls
## Week of January 11, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days; rice made for 9 that week — 1 leftover serving)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~50 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok

---

## Overview

Inspired by nasu dengaku — the classic Japanese dish of miso-glazed eggplant. Extended here with tofu for extra protein and turned into a full bowl meal. The miso glaze caramelizes beautifully in a hot wok, giving the eggplant a sticky, deeply savory-sweet coating. No cornstarch needed.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 2.5 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine rice

**Produce**
- 3–4 lbs eggplant (Japanese eggplant preferred; globe eggplant works too)
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 bunch green onions

**Miso Glaze**
- 4 tbsp white or red miso paste
- 3 tbsp mirin
- 2 tbsp sake (or dry sherry)
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp sesame oil

**Garnish**
- Sesame seeds
- Sliced green onions

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press 20+ min, then cut into 1-inch cubes or slabs.
- **Prep eggplant:** Cut into 1-inch cubes or half-moons. Salt eggplant pieces, let sit 15 min to draw out moisture, then pat dry with paper towels. (This prevents sogginess.)
- **Mix miso glaze:** Whisk miso + mirin + sake + sugar + soy sauce + sesame oil until smooth. No lumps. Set aside.
- **Mince garlic** and set aside.
- **Slice green onions** and set aside.
- **Cook rice** per package. Fluff and set aside.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook rice
Cook rice per package. Fluff and set aside.

### Step 2: Fry tofu (8–10 min)
Heat 2 tbsp neutral oil in wok over medium-high. Add tofu. Fry 3–4 min per side until golden. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Cook eggplant (8–10 min)
Add another tbsp of oil to wok. Add salted, dried eggplant. Stir-fry over high heat, pressing pieces against the wok surface. Cook until eggplant is golden and softened, about 8–10 min. Add garlic in the last minute.

### Step 4: Glaze (2–3 min)
Pour miso glaze over eggplant. Toss to coat and let it bubble and thicken for 1–2 min. Return tofu and toss to coat in the glaze as well.

### Step 5: Finish and portion
Remove from heat. Portion over rice. Top with sesame seeds and green onions.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days refrigerated.
- Eggplant softens further as it sits — this is fine and the flavor improves.
- Reheat in microwave or briefly in the wok.
