# Malaysian Nasi Lemak with Sambal Tofu
## Week of December 13, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~55 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok + rice cooker or pot

---

## Overview

Coconut rice (rice cooked in coconut milk with pandan) served with spicy sambal tofu, crispy fried shallots, cucumber, and roasted peanuts. You did laksa in July — this is the other essential Malaysian dish. The sambal (chili-shrimp paste relish) is the star — deeply savory, spicy, and slightly sweet.

**Ingredient chain:** Coconut milk carries from last week (or your pantry by now). Peanuts, shallots, and sambal ingredients are specific to this dish.

---

## Mise en Place

*Coconut rice:*
- 5 cups dry jasmine rice
- 2 cans (13.5 oz each) coconut milk
- 2 cups water (adjust based on coconut milk volume to match your rice cooker's water line)
- 2 pandan leaves, tied in a knot (or 1 tsp pandan extract) — optional but traditional
- 2-inch piece ginger, sliced
- 1 tsp salt

*Sambal (blend or process together):*
- 15 dried red chilies, soaked in hot water 15 min, drained
- 6 shallots
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp shrimp paste (belacan)
- 2 tbsp tamarind paste
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into triangles or 1-inch cubes

*Accompaniments:*
- 4 shallots, thinly sliced (for frying)
- 1 cup roasted peanuts
- 2 cucumbers, sliced
- 5 hard-boiled eggs, halved (optional)

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Start coconut rice
Combine **rice**, **coconut milk**, **water**, **pandan leaves**, **ginger slices**, and **salt** in a rice cooker or pot. Cook normally. When done, discard pandan and ginger, fluff with a fork.

### Step 2: Fry the shallots (5 min)
Heat **1/2 cup oil** in the wok over medium heat. Fry the **sliced shallots** until deep golden, 5–7 minutes. Remove with slotted spoon. Reserve oil.

### Step 3: Make the sambal (10 min)
Heat **3 tbsp shallot oil** in the wok over medium heat. Add the **blended sambal paste**. Fry, stirring often, for 8–10 minutes until the paste darkens, oil separates, and it's very fragrant.

### Step 4: Fry the tofu (8 min)
Push sambal to the side (or remove). Add more oil if needed. Fry the **tofu** until golden and crispy, about 4 minutes per batch. Toss the tofu in the sambal to coat.

### Step 5: Assemble
Portion **coconut rice** into containers. Add **sambal tofu**, **fried shallots**, **peanuts**, **cucumber slices**, and **halved eggs** (if using).

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- The coconut rice has a subtle fragrance that makes the whole meal feel special
- Sambal keeps for weeks in the fridge — make extra to use as a condiment
- Keep cucumber separate until serving for freshness
- Fried shallots lose crunch after day 1 — store separately in a paper-towel-lined container
