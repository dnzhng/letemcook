# Taiwanese Three Cup Tofu (三杯豆腐)
## Week of May 3, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~50 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok

---

## Overview

Three Cup Tofu gets its name from the three sauce components used in roughly equal parts: sesame oil, soy sauce, and Shaoxing rice wine. The sauce is bold, sticky, and slightly sweet — loaded with whole garlic cloves, ginger coins, and a mountain of fresh Thai basil stirred in at the very end off the heat. No cornstarch; the sauce reduces naturally into a glossy glaze. Classic Taiwanese comfort food.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine rice

**Aromatics**
- 12 cloves garlic, peeled and left whole (do not mince)
- 2-inch piece fresh ginger, sliced into coins (do not mince)
- 6–8 dried red chilies

**The Three Cups (sauce)**
- ⅓ cup toasted sesame oil
- ⅓ cup soy sauce
- ⅓ cup Shaoxing rice wine (or dry sherry)
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce (for color)

**Basil**
- 2 large bunches fresh Thai basil, leaves only *(critical — add OFF HEAT)*

**For cooking**
- 2 tbsp neutral oil

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press 20+ min, then cut into 1-inch cubes. Pat dry.
- **Peel garlic** — leave whole. Set aside.
- **Slice ginger into coins** — set aside with garlic.
- **Prep dried chilies** — break in half, shake out some seeds if desired.
- **Mix the three cups:** Combine sesame oil + soy sauce + Shaoxing wine + sugar + dark soy in one bowl. Stir until sugar is dissolved. Set aside.
- **Pick basil leaves** off stems — keep in a large bowl near the stove. This is added off-heat.
- **Cook rice** per package. Fluff and set aside.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook rice
Cook rice per package. Fluff and set aside.

### Step 2: Fry tofu (8–10 min)
Heat 2 tbsp neutral oil in wok over medium-high. Add tofu. Fry 3–4 min per side until golden and slightly crispy. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Fry garlic and ginger (3–4 min)
In the same wok over medium heat, add sesame oil from the sauce mixture (if you want to reserve it) or a drizzle of neutral oil. Add whole garlic cloves and ginger coins. Fry 3–4 min until edges are golden and the kitchen smells incredible.

### Step 4: Add chilies (30 sec)
Add dried chilies. Stir-fry 30 seconds.

### Step 5: Add sauce and tofu (5 min)
Return tofu to wok. Pour three-cup sauce over everything. Toss well. Bring to a simmer and cook 4–5 min uncovered, letting the sauce reduce and thicken into a glossy glaze.

### Step 6: Basil OFF HEAT ⚠️
Remove wok from heat completely. Add all the Thai basil. Toss quickly — the residual heat wilts the basil perfectly. Do NOT add basil while still over heat; it turns black and bitter.

### Step 7: Portion
Serve over rice immediately.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days. Basil will darken in containers — this is normal.
- The tofu absorbs the sauce as it sits; flavor is even better day 2–3.
- Reheat gently with a small splash of water.
- Sesame oil: toasted (dark) sesame oil is essential; untoasted will not give the right flavor.
