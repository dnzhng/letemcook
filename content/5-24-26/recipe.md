# Multi-Meal Week: Tikka / Japanese Curry / Mapo Tofu & Drunken Noodles
## Week of May 24, 2026

**Servings:** 10 total — 2 tikka + 2 curry + 6 mapo/drunken noodles
**Active Time:** ~35 min (plus a bulk mise en place session of ~35 min)
**Total Time:** ~70 min across all prep and cook
**Equipment:** Single wok

---

## Overview

A leftovers-cleanup week. Three smaller meals built around what was in the fridge: leftover tikka masala sauce, leftover Japanese curry, a large batch of garlic and Thai chilies. The star of the week is Sichuan-style Mapo Tofu / Pad Kee Mao (drunken noodles) — built from the aromatics on hand.

The bulk mise en place session handles everything at once so each cook night is a fast throw-and-stir.

---

## BULK MISE EN PLACE (Do all at once — ~35 min)

This session preps all three dishes simultaneously.

- **Cook all rice:** 5 cups dry jasmine rice total. Cook in batches or a large pot. Spread on trays to cool. Refrigerate uncovered.
- **Cook noodles:** 2 lbs wide rice noodles per package. Drain, toss with a drizzle of neutral oil. Refrigerate.
- **Blanch tofu (for Mapo):** Cube 2 blocks tofu into 1-inch pieces. Blanch in lightly salted boiling water 3 min. Drain carefully. Refrigerate. (This pre-firms the tofu so it holds up in the mapo braise.)
- **Sear chicken for tikka:** Cut 4 chicken thighs into chunks. Season with salt and garam masala. Sear in wok 4–5 min until cooked through. Set aside.
- **Toast and grind Sichuan peppercorns:** Toast 2 tbsp in a dry pan 2 min. Grind in a spice grinder or mortar. Use in Mapo; reserve some for serving.
- **Mix all aromatics:** Mince together all remaining garlic (leftover) + 2-inch knob ginger + all remaining Thai chilies (leftover). Divide into two containers: one for Mapo (larger portion), one remaining.
- **Mix Mapo sauce:** Combine in a jar — 2 tbsp doubanjiang + 3 tbsp Shaoxing wine + 3 tbsp soy sauce + 1 tbsp dark soy + ½ tsp sugar + 1 tbsp toasted Sichuan peppercorn powder. Seal and refrigerate.

**After mise en place, your fridge should contain:**
- ✅ All rice, cooked and cooling
- ✅ All noodles, cooked and oiled
- ✅ Blanched Mapo tofu
- ✅ Seared chicken
- ✅ Minced aromatics (divided)
- ✅ Mapo sauce (in a jar)
- ✅ Ground Sichuan peppercorn

# DISH 1: Chicken Tikka Masala over Rice (2 servings)

## Additional Ingredients
- Leftover tikka masala sauce — 2 servings worth
- Cooked rice from mise en place — 2 portions

## Steps
1. Warm leftover tikka masala sauce in wok over medium heat.
2. Add seared chicken from mise en place. Simmer 5 min.
3. Serve over rice. Done in 15 min.

---

# DISH 2: Japanese Curry with Crispy Tofu over Rice (2 servings)

## Additional Ingredients
- Leftover Japanese curry — 2 servings worth
- ½ block extra-firm tofu, cubed
- 1 small onion, sliced
- Cooked rice from mise en place — 2 portions

## Steps
1. Fry tofu cubes in wok with 1 tbsp oil until golden, 5–6 min. Remove.
2. Sauté onion slices 3 min.
3. Add leftover curry. Warm through 3–4 min.
4. Return tofu. Stir to combine.
5. Serve over rice. Done in 20 min.

---

# DISH 3: Sichuan Mapo Tofu over Rice (6 servings)

## Additional Ingredients
- Blanched tofu from mise en place — 2 blocks
- Mapo aromatics from mise en place — large portion
- Mapo sauce jar from mise en place
- 2.5 cups vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- Green onions — 1 bunch, sliced
- Cooked rice from mise en place — 6 portions

## Steps

### Step 1: Fry aromatics (2 min)
Heat wok over high. Add 2 tbsp neutral oil. Add Mapo aromatics (garlic + ginger + Thai chilies). Stir-fry 2 min.

### Step 2: Add sauce (1 min)
Pour in Mapo sauce jar. Stir 30 seconds — it will sizzle and darken.

### Step 3: Add stock and tofu (10–12 min)
Add 2.5 cups stock. Bring to a simmer. Gently slide in pre-blanched tofu. Don't stir vigorously — push sauce around tofu with the back of a spoon. Braise at a steady simmer 10–12 min, swirling occasionally. Sauce will reduce by about half and naturally thicken.

### Step 4: Finish
Sprinkle 1.5 tsp ground Sichuan peppercorn over the top. Add green onion greens. Drizzle 1 tbsp sesame oil. Swirl once, kill the heat.

### Step 5: Portion
Plate over rice. Sprinkle remaining Sichuan peppercorn powder over individual servings.

## Meal Prep Notes

- All three dishes store up to 4–5 days.
- Mapo sauce thickens in the fridge — add a splash of water when reheating.
- The bulk mise en place turns each subsequent cook into a 15–20 min throw-together.
