# Malaysian Tofu Laksa
## Week of July 5, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~50 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

A rich, spicy coconut curry noodle soup with crispy fried tofu puffs, bean sprouts, and a fragrant laksa paste. Malaysian laksa is completely different from Thai curries — it brings galangal, candlenut, dried shrimp, and shrimp paste into the mix for a deeper, more complex flavor. This is your first Malaysian dish.

**Ingredient chain:** Coconut milk and limes carry forward to next week's Thai Green Curry.

---

## Mise en Place

**Prep these first and set aside:**

*Laksa paste (combine in a blender or food processor):*
- 6 dried red chilies, soaked in hot water 15 min and drained
- 8 shallots, roughly chopped
- 6 cloves garlic
- 3-inch piece galangal (or ginger), sliced
- 3 stalks lemongrass (bottom 4 inches, sliced)
- 1 tbsp shrimp paste (belacan)
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (to help blend)

*Toppings (prep and set in bowls):*
- 4 cups bean sprouts, rinsed
- 1 bunch fresh cilantro, roughly chopped
- 1 bunch fresh mint leaves
- 5 limes, cut into wedges
- Sambal oelek for serving

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks (14–16 oz each) extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into 1-inch cubes

*Noodles:*
- 1.5 lbs dried rice vermicelli

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Fry the tofu (10 min)
Heat 3 tbsp neutral oil in the wok over high heat. Fry the **tofu cubes** in two batches until golden on all sides, about 4–5 minutes per batch. Remove to a plate lined with paper towels. Set aside.

### Step 2: Cook the laksa paste (5 min)
In the same wok (drain excess oil, leave about 2 tbsp), reduce heat to medium. Add the **entire blended laksa paste**. Stir-fry for 4–5 minutes until very fragrant and the oil starts to separate from the paste.

### Step 3: Build the broth (15 min)
Pour in **4 cans (13.5 oz each) coconut milk** and **4 cups water or vegetable stock**. Stir well to combine with the paste. Add **3 tbsp fish sauce**, **1 tbsp sugar**, and **1 tsp salt**. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce to a simmer for 10 minutes. Taste and adjust — it should be rich, spicy, and slightly sweet.

### Step 4: Cook the noodles (5 min)
While the broth simmers, cook **1.5 lbs rice vermicelli** according to package directions (usually just soak in boiling water for 3–5 minutes). Drain and rinse with cold water.

### Step 5: Assemble
Return the **fried tofu cubes** to the broth and simmer 2 minutes to warm through. Divide noodles among containers. Ladle broth and tofu over noodles. Top with **bean sprouts**, **cilantro**, **mint**, and a **lime wedge**.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Store broth/tofu and noodles in separate containers — noodles get mushy if stored in liquid
- Reheat broth in microwave or stovetop, pour over cold noodles
- Bean sprouts don't keep well past day 3 — add fresh if possible, or sub shredded cabbage
- The broth actually improves overnight as the flavors meld
