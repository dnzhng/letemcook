# Greek Lemon Rice with Chickpeas & Crispy Herbed Tofu
## Week of May 31, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~40 minutes
**Total Time:** ~75 minutes (includes 30 min tofu press)
**Equipment:** Single large wok with lid (or foil to cover)

---

## Overview

A bright, summery one-wok dinner: crispy oregano-lemon tofu cubes set atop fluffy lemon-scented rice studded with chickpeas, kalamata olives, wilted spinach, and a generous shower of fresh dill and parsley. A total flavor reset after a spring of heavy stews, curries, and stir-fries. The wok does everything — tofu first, rice built in the same pan. Most unusual purchase: kalamata olives.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each)
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine or long-grain rice
- 2 cans chickpeas (15 oz each), drained and rinsed

**Produce**
- 4 lemons (juice + zest)
- 10 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 large yellow onion, diced
- 5 oz baby spinach
- 1 cup kalamata olives, pitted and halved

**Herbs**
- 1 bunch fresh dill, roughly chopped
- 1 bunch fresh parsley, roughly chopped
- 2 tsp dried oregano

**Liquids**
- 3 cups vegetable broth

**Tofu Herb Crust**
- Zest of 2 lemons
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Salt and pepper
- 2 tbsp olive oil

**For cooking**
- 3 tbsp olive oil total

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press 30+ min, then cube into 1-inch pieces.
- **Make tofu crust mix:** Combine lemon zest + oregano + garlic powder + salt + pepper + 2 tbsp olive oil in a bowl. Toss tofu cubes in mixture until coated. Set aside.
- **Mince garlic** and **dice onion** — keep separate.
- **Zest and juice lemons:** Zest goes on the tofu; juice goes in with the rice.
- **Drain and rinse chickpeas.**
- **Pit and halve olives** (if not pre-pitted).
- **Measure broth** and set aside.
- **Pick and roughly chop fresh dill and parsley** — keep refrigerated until serving.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Sear tofu (10–12 min)
Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in wok over medium-high. Add herbed tofu in a single layer (work in batches). Sear 4–5 min per side without moving until golden. The lemon zest will caramelize and darken slightly — this is good. Remove and set aside.

### Step 2: Build lemon-chickpea rice (8 min)
In the same wok over medium heat, add 1 tbsp olive oil. Add onion and cook 4–5 min until soft. Add garlic and cook 2 more min. Add dried oregano and stir 30 seconds.

### Step 3: Toast rice (2 min)
Add dry rice to the wok. Stir to coat in oil and aromatics. Toast 1–2 min until rice smells nutty.

### Step 4: Add liquid and simmer (20–22 min)
Add vegetable broth + juice of 4 lemons. Stir in chickpeas. Bring to a boil, then reduce to low. Cover tightly and simmer 20–22 min until rice is cooked and liquid is absorbed. Do not lift the lid.

### Step 5: Rest and wilt spinach (5 min)
Remove from heat with lid still on — rest 5 min. Remove lid and scatter spinach over the top. Replace lid for 1–2 min; spinach will wilt from residual steam.

### Step 6: Combine and portion
Fluff rice and stir wilted spinach through. Fold in olives. Portion into containers. Top with crispy herbed tofu. Scatter fresh dill and parsley generously over the top.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Keep dill and parsley separate if possible — sprinkle fresh when eating for brightness.
- Stores up to 5 days refrigerated.
- Reheat in microwave; add a squeeze of fresh lemon when serving if available.
- The olives and herbs are what make this feel special — don't skip them.
