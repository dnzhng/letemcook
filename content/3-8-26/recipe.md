# Spicy Pork Belly Fried Rice
## Week of March 8, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~35 minutes
**Total Time:** ~45 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok

---

## Overview

An improvised week using on-hand ingredients — pork belly, eggs, portobello mushrooms, bean sprouts, spinach, and shallots over rice. Kimchi was kept on the side rather than cooked in; oyster sauce + soy provide the savory backbone. The pork belly fat renders into the rice and makes everything taste incredible.

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- Pork belly — as much as available (slice thin, ¼-inch)
- 4 eggs, beaten
- 3.5 cups dry jasmine rice (cook ahead, cold)

**Produce**
- 2 portobello mushrooms, diced
- 2 cups bean sprouts
- 3 cups fresh spinach
- 3 shallots, thinly sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 Thai chilies, thinly sliced (adjust to heat)
- 1 bunch green onions

**Sauce**
- 3 tbsp oyster sauce *(key purchase that week)*
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp sugar
- Optional: 1 tbsp rice vinegar for brightness

**To serve (on the side)**
- Kimchi — ¼–½ cup per serving, cold

---

## Mise en Place

- **Cook rice 1–2 days ahead:** Spread cooked rice on a tray, cool completely, refrigerate uncovered. Cold rice is essential for fried rice.
- **Slice pork belly thin** (¼-inch). Pat dry.
- **Dice mushrooms**, **slice shallots**, **mince garlic**, **slice chilies** — combine all in one bowl.
- **Beat eggs** in a small bowl.
- **Mix sauce:** Stir together oyster sauce + soy sauce + sesame oil + sugar (+ vinegar if using). Set aside.
- **Measure bean sprouts** and **spinach** — keep separate.
- **Slice green onions** and set aside.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Render and crisp pork belly (8–10 min)
Heat wok over medium. Add pork belly slices — no oil needed, the fat will render. Cook 3–4 min per side until the fat is translucent and edges are crispy. Remove pork and set aside. Leave rendered fat in the wok.

### Step 2: Scramble eggs (2 min)
In the pork fat, pour in beaten eggs. Scramble until just set. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Fry aromatics and mushrooms (4 min)
Add shallots, garlic, chilies, and diced mushrooms to the wok (still with some rendered fat). Stir-fry over high heat 3–4 min until mushrooms are golden.

### Step 4: Fry rice (4–5 min)
Add cold rice. Press and toss, letting it sit against the hot wok surface to get some color. Repeat 3–4 times.

### Step 5: Sauce and combine (2 min)
Pour sauce over rice. Toss well. Return pork belly and eggs. Add bean sprouts and toss briefly — they should stay slightly crunchy. Add spinach and fold in — it will wilt quickly.

### Step 6: Finish and portion
Add green onions. Taste and adjust soy sauce or oyster sauce. Serve with kimchi on the side.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Store kimchi separately — always serve cold on the side.
- Stores up to 4 days refrigerated.
- Reheat in microwave or briefly in the wok.
