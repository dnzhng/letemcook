# Kung Pao Tofu (宫保豆腐)
## Week of July 26, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~30 minutes
**Total Time:** ~50 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

Crispy tofu, roasted peanuts, and dried chilies in a tangy-sweet-spicy sauce with Sichuan peppercorn. The sauce is built from soy, black vinegar, and sugar — it reduces into a glossy glaze with no cornstarch. The Sichuan holy trinity: mapo last week, kung pao this week, dan dan later.

**Ingredient chain:** Uses leftover doubanjiang, Sichuan peppercorn, and scallions from last week's Mapo Tofu. Peanuts carry to next week's West African Peanut Stew.

---

## Mise en Place

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into 3/4-inch cubes

*Aromatics (prep together):*
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- 2-inch piece ginger, minced
- 1 bunch scallions (from last week's buy), whites and greens separated, sliced
- 15–20 dried red chilies (snipped in half, seeds shaken out for less heat)

*Vegetables:*
- 3 bell peppers, cut into 3/4-inch pieces
- 1 cup roasted unsalted peanuts

*Kung Pao sauce (whisk together in a bowl):*
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp Chinkiang black vinegar (or rice vinegar)
- 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine (from last week)
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp doubanjiang (from last week's jar)
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1/2 cup water

*Finishing:*
- 1 tsp ground Sichuan peppercorn (from last week)

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry jasmine rice

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice
Start **5 cups dry jasmine rice**.

### Step 2: Fry the tofu (8 min)
Heat **3 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Fry the **tofu cubes** in two batches until crispy and golden on all sides, about 4 minutes per batch. Remove to a plate.

### Step 3: Toast the chilies and aromatics (2 min)
Reduce heat to medium. Add **1 tbsp oil** to the wok. Add the **dried chilies** and stir for 30 seconds until darkened and fragrant (don't burn). Add the **garlic**, **ginger**, and **scallion whites**. Stir-fry 1 minute.

### Step 4: Cook the vegetables (3 min)
Increase heat to high. Add the **bell peppers**. Stir-fry for 2–3 minutes until slightly charred but still crisp.

### Step 5: Sauce and finish (3 min)
Return the **tofu** to the wok. Pour in the **kung pao sauce**. Toss everything together and let the sauce bubble and reduce for 2–3 minutes until glossy and clinging to the tofu. Add the **peanuts** and **scallion greens**. Sprinkle **1 tsp ground Sichuan peppercorn**. Toss once more.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Add peanuts fresh at serving for maximum crunch, or store them in the container (they'll soften slightly but stay tasty)
- The sauce continues to reduce as it cools — this is fine, it becomes more concentrated
- Reheats well in microwave or wok
