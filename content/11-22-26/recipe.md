# Chinese Char Siu–Glazed Tofu (叉烧豆腐)
## Week of November 22, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~25 minutes
**Total Time:** ~50 minutes (includes 20 min marination)
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

BBQ-glazed tofu with hoisin, five-spice, honey, and soy sauce. The glaze caramelizes in the wok into a sticky, sweet-savory lacquer — think Chinese BBQ pork but with tofu. Serve over rice with stir-fried bok choy. You've done several Sichuan dishes — this is Cantonese, sweeter and more aromatic.

**Ingredient chain:** Clean start. Five-spice powder carries to December's Taiwanese Braised Soy Tofu.

---

## Mise en Place

*Char siu marinade/glaze (whisk together):*
- 4 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp Chinese five-spice powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp white pepper
- Few drops red food coloring (optional but traditional)

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into thick rectangular slabs (like pork belly slices)
- Marinate in half the char siu glaze for 20 min (reserve the other half for basting)

*Bok choy:*
- 2 lbs baby bok choy, halved
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp soy sauce

*Rice:*
- 5 cups dry jasmine rice

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Cook rice
Start **5 cups dry jasmine rice**.

### Step 2: Sear and glaze the tofu (12 min)
Heat **3 tbsp neutral oil** in the wok over high heat. Remove **tofu slabs** from marinade (reserve marinade). Sear in batches, 3–4 minutes per side until deeply golden and slightly charred. In the last minute, spoon the **reserved glaze** over each piece and let it caramelize, turning once. The glaze should bubble, darken, and become sticky. Remove to a plate.

### Step 3: Stir-fry bok choy (4 min)
Add **1 tbsp oil** to the wok. Add the **garlic** and stir 30 seconds. Add the **bok choy** halves cut-side down. Let them char 2 minutes without moving. Flip, add **1 tbsp soy sauce** and **2 tbsp water**. Cover 1 minute until bright green and just tender.

### Step 4: Serve
Portion rice. Arrange **bok choy** beside the rice. Slice the **char siu tofu** and fan over the rice. Drizzle any remaining pan glaze on top.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- The glaze keeps the tofu moist during storage and reheating
- The lacquered exterior will soften slightly overnight but the flavor only improves
- For extra caramelization, broil the stored portions for 2 minutes before eating
