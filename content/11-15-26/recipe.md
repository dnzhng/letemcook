# Lebanese Mujaddara with Crispy Tofu
## Week of November 15, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~30 minutes
**Total Time:** ~60 minutes
**Equipment:** Single large wok

---

## Overview

Lentils and rice cooked together, topped with deeply caramelized onions (almost blackened) and crispy tofu. Served with a simple lemon-yogurt sauce. The caramelized onions make this dish extraordinary — they're sweet, jammy, almost like onion candy. Cheap, hearty, and your first Lebanese dish. Perfect cold-weather comfort.

**Ingredient chain:** Uses leftover onions from last week's Massaman Curry. Onions are the star — you need a LOT for the caramelized topping.

---

## Mise en Place

*Caramelized onions (the star — prep first):*
- 5 large yellow onions (3 from last week + 2 new), halved and sliced into thin half-moons
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- Pinch of salt

*Lentils and rice:*
- 2 cups brown or green lentils, rinsed
- 3 cups long-grain white rice, rinsed
- 7 cups water or vegetable stock
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp allspice
- 1.5 tsp salt

*Tofu:*
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu, pressed 20 min, cut into 3/4-inch cubes

*Yogurt sauce:*
- 2 cups plain yogurt (Greek or regular)
- Juice of 2 lemons
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- Pinch of salt

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Start the caramelized onions (30 min — do first, cook low and slow)
Heat **4 tbsp olive oil** in the wok over medium heat. Add **all the sliced onions** and a **pinch of salt**. Cook, stirring every 5 minutes, for 25–30 minutes until deeply dark brown, sweet, and jammy. Don't rush this — low and slow is the whole point. Remove and set aside (reserve 2 tbsp of the onion oil in the wok).

### Step 2: Cook the lentils (during onion time, use a separate pot)
In a pot, combine **2 cups lentils** with **4 cups water**. Bring to a boil, reduce to simmer, cook 15–20 minutes until lentils are just tender but still hold their shape. Drain.

### Step 3: Cook rice with lentils (20 min)
In the same wok (after removing onions), add **1 tbsp olive oil**. Toast **3 cups rice** with the **cumin**, **coriander**, **cinnamon**, and **allspice** for 2 minutes. Add the **cooked lentils** and **5 cups water/stock** and **salt**. Bring to a boil, cover tightly (use foil if no lid), reduce to lowest heat. Cook 18–20 minutes until rice is tender and water is absorbed.

### Step 4: Sear the tofu (8 min — during rice cook time)
In a separate pan (or wait and use the wok after rice), heat **3 tbsp olive oil** over high heat. Fry the **tofu cubes** until golden and crispy, about 4 minutes per batch.

### Step 5: Make yogurt sauce
Whisk together **yogurt**, **lemon juice**, **garlic**, and **salt**.

### Step 6: Serve
Fluff the lentil-rice. Portion into containers. Top with a generous mound of **caramelized onions**, **crispy tofu**, and a drizzle of **yogurt sauce**.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- This is a dish where simplicity is the strength — don't skip or rush the caramelized onions
- The lentil-rice keeps extremely well and actually improves in texture overnight
- Yogurt sauce in a separate container to keep things from getting soggy
- Extremely budget-friendly — lentils, rice, onions, and tofu for 10 servings
