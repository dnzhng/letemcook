# Tofu Tikka Masala with Basmati Rice
## Week of March 1, 2026

**Servings:** 10 (2/day × 5 days)
**Active Time:** ~40 minutes
**Total Time:** ~60 minutes
**Equipment:** Single wok or large deep pan

---

## Overview

A quick, highly-rated tofu tikka masala adapted from Pick Up Limes. The sauce is coconut cream + yogurt + tomato + blended cashews — rich, creamy, and warming. Unlike a lot of tikka masala recipes, no cornstarch is needed; the cashews and coconut cream thicken naturally. Flavors improve significantly overnight. About 80–90 oz of sauce total (~8–9 oz per serving before adding tofu).

---

## Ingredients (10 servings)

**Protein & Grain**
- 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14–16 oz each, ~2.75 lbs total)
- 3.5 cups dry basmati rice

**Produce**
- 5 medium yellow onions, diced
- 3–4 inch piece fresh ginger, grated
- 5 large fresh tomatoes (or 2 cans diced tomatoes, 14 oz each)
- 3 limes
- 1 bunch fresh cilantro (optional garnish)

**Dairy & Sauce**
- 2 cans (13.5 oz each) coconut cream
- 2.5 cups plain yogurt (or coconut yogurt for vegan)
- 1 cup tomato paste
- 1 cup raw cashews (blended into sauce)

**Spices**
- 3 tbsp garam masala
- 2 tbsp ground cumin
- 2 tbsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cayenne (adjust to heat)
- Salt to taste

**For cooking**
- 3 tbsp neutral oil

---

## Mise en Place

- **Press tofu:** Press 20+ min, then cut into 1-inch cubes.
- **Soak cashews:** Cover cashews in hot water for 15 min, then drain. (Speeds up blending.)
- **Blend cashews:** Blend soaked cashews with ½ cup water until completely smooth. Set aside.
- **Dice onions** and set aside.
- **Grate ginger** and set aside.
- **Dice tomatoes** (or open cans) and set aside.
- **Measure all spices** into one small bowl: garam masala + cumin + coriander + turmeric + paprika + cayenne.
- **Cook basmati rice** per package. Fluff and set aside.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Cook rice
Cook basmati rice per package. Fluff and set aside.

### Step 2: Fry tofu (8–10 min)
Heat 2 tbsp oil in wok over medium-high. Add tofu cubes. Fry 3–4 min per side until golden. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Build the sauce base (10 min)
In the same wok over medium heat, add 1 tbsp oil. Add onions. Cook, stirring occasionally, 8–10 min until deeply golden and softened.

### Step 4: Add spices and ginger (2 min)
Add ginger. Stir 1 min. Add all spices. Stir constantly 1 min until fragrant and darkened.

### Step 5: Add tomatoes (5 min)
Add tomatoes and tomato paste. Cook 5 min, stirring occasionally, until tomatoes break down and mixture thickens.

### Step 6: Add dairy and cashews (10 min)
Add coconut cream + yogurt + blended cashews. Stir well. Bring to a gentle simmer. Cook 8–10 min until sauce is thick and creamy. Season with lime juice and salt.

### Step 7: Return tofu (5 min)
Add tofu to the sauce. Simmer 5 min so tofu absorbs the flavors.

### Step 8: Portion
Serve over basmati rice. Garnish with cilantro and a lime wedge.

---

## Meal Prep Notes

- Stores up to 5 days. **This recipe is better on day 2–3** — the sauce seeps into the tofu.
- Sauce keeps everything moist when reheating — no dry tofu problem.
- Reheat in microwave 90–120 seconds, stirring halfway.
