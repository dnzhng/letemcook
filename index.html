Smoky Paprika Tofu with Tomato Rice

Week of January 19, 2026

Makes: 10 servings (5 days × 2 servings)
Prep time: 20 minutes
Cook time: 45 minutes
Total time: 65 minutes

What Makes This Special

This dish brings the warm, smoky flavors of Spain to your meal prep! The star ingredient is pimentón (Spanish smoked paprika), which gives the tofu an incredible depth of flavor. Combined with Spanish-style tomato rice (similar to arroz con tomate), you get a complete meal that's completely different from anything you've made recently.

Unlike your recent Asian cuisines, this has Mediterranean vibes with:

  • Smoky, earthy paprika-roasted tofu that gets wonderfully crispy
  • Aromatic tomato rice cooked in the Spanish style with bell peppers
  • Simple ingredients but bold, memorable flavors
  • No wok needed - oven-roasted tofu and stovetop rice

Ingredients

For the Smoky Paprika Tofu:

  • 2.5 lbs extra-firm tofu (2-3 blocks)
  • 4 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 tablespoons smoked paprika (Spanish pimentón dulce)
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

For the Spanish Tomato Rice:

  • 4 cups long-grain white rice (uncooked)
  • 3 medium yellow onions, diced
  • 8-10 cloves garlic, minced
  • 6-7 large tomatoes, diced (or 2 cans, 28 oz each, diced tomatoes)
  • 3 medium bell peppers (red or yellow), diced
  • 1/4 cup tomato paste
  • 7 cups vegetable broth
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons dried oregano
  • 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

Step 1: Prepare the Tofu (20 minutes)

  1. Preheat oven to 480°F (250°C) - use your highest setting with bake mode.
  2. Press the tofu: Remove tofu from packaging and pat very dry with paper towels. Press down firmly with paper towels to remove as much liquid as possible. The drier the tofu, the crispier it will get!
  3. Cut tofu: Cut each block in half lengthwise, then cut into 3/4-inch cubes.
  4. Season the tofu: In a large bowl, combine 4 tablespoons olive oil, 3 tablespoons smoked paprika, 1 teaspoon garlic powder, 1 teaspoon onion powder, 1 teaspoon salt, and 1/2 teaspoon black pepper.
  5. Coat tofu: Add cubed tofu to the bowl and toss gently but thoroughly until every piece is well coated with the paprika mixture.
  6. Arrange on baking sheet: Line 2 large baking sheets with parchment paper or foil. Spread tofu in a single layer - don't overcrowd! Leave space between pieces for maximum crispiness.
  7. Bake: Place in preheated oven and bake for 15 minutes. The tofu should be crispy on the outside with beautiful red color from the paprika.
  8. Set aside: Remove from oven and let cool slightly while you make the rice.

Step 2: Make the Spanish Tomato Rice (45 minutes)

  1. Heat oil: In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat 3 tablespoons olive oil over medium heat.
  2. Sauté aromatics: Add diced onions and cook for 5-7 minutes until softened and starting to turn golden. Add minced garlic and cook for 1 minute more until fragrant.
  3. Toast the rice: Add the 4 cups of uncooked rice to the pot. Stir constantly for 2-3 minutes until the rice starts to smell toasty and turns slightly golden. This step is crucial for fluffy rice!
  4. Add tomato paste and paprika: Stir in the tomato paste and 1 tablespoon smoked paprika. Cook for 30 seconds, stirring constantly.
  5. Add vegetables and liquids: Add diced tomatoes, diced bell peppers, 7 cups vegetable broth, 2 teaspoons dried oregano, and salt and pepper to taste.
  6. Bring to boil: Stir everything together and bring to a rolling boil over high heat.
  7. Simmer: Once boiling, reduce heat to LOW, cover with a tight-fitting lid, and simmer for 20 minutes. DO NOT LIFT THE LID OR STIR! This is critical for fluffy rice.
  8. Rest: After 20 minutes, remove from heat but keep the lid on. Let it sit covered for 10 more minutes to steam and finish cooking.
  9. Fluff: Remove lid and gently fluff the rice with a fork. The rice should be tender and have absorbed all the liquid.

Step 3: Combine and Portion (5 minutes)

  1. Mix gently: Add the crispy paprika tofu to the pot of rice. Gently fold together, being careful not to break up the tofu too much.
  2. Taste and adjust: Taste and add more salt, pepper, or paprika if needed.
  3. Garnish: Sprinkle with freshly chopped parsley.
  4. Portion: Divide into 10 equal portions in your meal prep containers.

Storage & Reheating

Storage: Let cool completely before sealing containers. Store in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.

Reheating:

  • Microwave: 2-3 minutes on high, stirring halfway through. Add a splash of water if it seems dry.
  • Stovetop: Reheat in a pan over medium heat with a splash of water or broth, stirring occasionally, 5-7 minutes.

Pro tip: The flavors actually improve after a day in the fridge as everything marinates together!

Tips for Success

Crispy Tofu:

  • Press tofu REALLY well - the drier it is, the crispier it gets
  • Use your oven's highest temperature
  • Don't overcrowd the baking sheet
  • Spanish smoked paprika (pimentón) gives the best flavor - look for "dulce" (sweet) or "agridulce" (bittersweet)

Perfect Rice:

  • Toasting the rice before adding liquid is crucial for fluffy, separate grains
  • Use a 1:1.75 rice-to-liquid ratio (4 cups rice to 7 cups broth)
  • DON'T lift the lid while simmering!
  • Let it rest covered after cooking
  • If rice seems too dry after cooking, add a splash more broth
  • If there's still liquid after 20 minutes, cook uncovered for 2-3 more minutes

Flavor Variations:

  • Add Spanish olives for a briny kick
  • Mix in chickpeas for extra protein
  • Top with a fried egg when reheating
  • Serve with a side of cucumber salad for freshness
  • Add a squeeze of lemon juice before eating

Shopping Notes

Essential ingredient: Spanish smoked paprika (pimentón) is what makes this dish special. It's different from regular paprika - it has a deep, smoky flavor that's irreplaceable. Look for it in the spice aisle or international section. Brands like La Chinata or La Dalia are excellent.
