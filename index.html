🇨🇺 Cuban Black Beans & Rice

Frijoles Negros with White Rice

Week of March 31, 2026

Servings: 10
Prep time: 15 minutes
Cook time: 45 minutes
Total time: ~60 minutes

Overview

Classic Cuban-style black beans (frijoles negros) slow-simmered with sofrito — a fragrant base of onion, bell pepper, garlic, and cumin — until thick and deeply savory. Served over fluffy white rice.

Simple pantry ingredients, big flavor. The beans thicken naturally into a rich, saucy stew perfect for meal prep.

Ingredients

Beans:

  • 4 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 cup water

Sofrito Base:

  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 medium yellow onions, finely diced
  • 2 green bell peppers, finely diced
  • 8 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Finishing:

  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar

White Rice:

  • 4 cups long-grain white rice
  • 5½ cups water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Garnish (optional):

  • Fresh cilantro, chopped (1 bunch)
  • Lime wedges (2-3 limes)

Instructions

Step 1: Make the Sofrito (10 min)

  1. Heat 3 tbsp olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat.
  2. Add 2 diced onions and 2 diced green bell peppers. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and lightly golden, about 7-8 minutes.
  3. Add 8 cloves minced garlic, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp oregano, and ½ tsp smoked paprika. Stir for 1 minute until fragrant.

Step 2: Build the Beans (5 min)

  1. Add the 4 cans of drained black beans, 2 cups vegetable broth, 1 cup water, and 1 bay leaf.
  2. Stir to combine and bring to a boil.

Step 3: Simmer (30 min)

  1. Reduce heat to medium-low. Simmer uncovered for 25-30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  2. Use the back of a spoon to mash about ¼ of the beans against the side of the pot — this thickens the sauce naturally.
  3. The beans are ready when the liquid has reduced to a thick, glossy sauce.
  4. Remove bay leaf. Stir in 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar and 1 tsp sugar. Taste and adjust salt.

Step 4: Cook the Rice (25 min, parallel)

  1. While beans simmer, rinse 4 cups white rice until water runs clear.
  2. Combine rice with 5½ cups water, 1 tsp salt, and 1 tbsp olive oil in a saucepan.
  3. Bring to a boil, then reduce to lowest heat, cover tightly, and cook for 18 minutes.
  4. Remove from heat and let sit covered for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork.

Step 5: Portion for Meal Prep

  1. Divide rice among 10 containers.
  2. Ladle beans generously over the rice.
  3. Top with cilantro if using. Add lime wedges on the side.

Meal Prep Notes

  • Reheating: Microwave 2-3 minutes. Add a splash of water if beans look dry — they thicken in the fridge.
  • Gets better with time: Flavors deepen significantly by day 2.
  • Storage: Keeps well in the fridge for 5 days.
  • Freezer-friendly: Beans (without rice) freeze well for up to 3 months.

Notes

  • The sofrito is the foundation. Don't rush it — taking time to properly cook the onions and peppers builds the flavor base everything else rests on.
  • Mash some beans. Smashing a quarter of the beans thickens the sauce without any starch. It's the key to that rich, restaurant-style consistency.
  • Vinegar at the end. A splash of apple cider vinegar brightens the whole dish — add it off the heat so the flavor stays sharp.
  • Green bell pepper, not red. Traditional Cuban sofrito uses green bell pepper for its slightly bitter, grassy flavor. It makes a difference.
