🇲🇦 Moroccan-Spiced Tofu

with Chickpeas & Couscous

Week of March 9, 2026

Servings: 10
Active time: ~35 minutes
Total time: ~50 minutes
Equipment: Wok + kettle

Overview

A North African-inspired braise — warm spices (cumin, coriander, cinnamon, smoked paprika), chickpeas, dried apricots, and green olives simmered in a tomato-based sauce with pan-fried tofu. Served over couscous, which takes 5 minutes to prepare.

No cornstarch, no oven, no fuss. The sweet-savory-spiced sauce is completely different from anything in your recent rotation.

Spice Blend (mix in advance)

  • 1 tbsp + 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp ground coriander
  • 1½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • ½ tsp cayenne pepper (adjust to taste)
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper

Ingredients

Tofu:

  • 3 blocks extra-firm tofu (14-16 oz each, ~2.75 lbs total)
  • 3 tbsp olive oil for pan-frying

Sauce & Vegetables:

  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 medium yellow onions, diced
  • 8 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced
  • The entire spice blend (above)
  • 2 cans (28 oz each) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup dried apricots, roughly chopped
  • ½ cup pitted green olives, halved
  • 2 cups vegetable broth or water
  • 2 tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice (about 1 lemon)

Couscous:

  • 4 cups dry couscous
  • 4 cups boiling water
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp salt

Garnish:

  • Fresh cilantro, roughly chopped (1 bunch)
  • Lemon wedges

Instructions

Step 1: Press and Cut Tofu (10 min)

  1. Drain all 3 blocks of tofu.
  2. Wrap each in paper towels and press under a heavy pan for 10 minutes.
  3. Cut into 1-inch cubes.

Step 2: Pan-Fry Tofu (10 min)

  1. Heat wok over medium-high heat with 3 tbsp olive oil.
  2. Working in 2-3 batches, pan-fry tofu until golden on 2-3 sides, about 3-4 minutes per batch.
  3. Don't crowd the wok — you want a sear, not steam.
  4. Remove to a plate and set aside.

Step 3: Build the Aromatic Base (8 min)

  1. In the same wok, add 3 tbsp olive oil over medium heat.
  2. Add 3 diced onions and cook until softened and golden, about 5 minutes.
  3. Add 8 cloves minced garlic and 1 tbsp minced ginger, stir for 1 minute.
  4. Add the entire spice blend and stir constantly for 1 minute — this blooms the spices.

Step 4: Build the Sauce (5 min)

  1. Pour in 2 cans crushed tomatoes and 2 cups vegetable broth.
  2. Stir in 2 cans chickpeas, 1 cup chopped dried apricots, ½ cup halved green olives, and 2 tbsp honey.
  3. Bring to a gentle boil.

Step 5: Braise (12 min)

  1. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer uncovered for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  2. The sauce will thicken and the apricots will soften.
  3. Return all pan-fried tofu to the wok, fold gently, and simmer for 2-3 more minutes.
  4. Finish with 2 tbsp lemon juice and taste for salt.

Step 6: Make Couscous (5 min)

  1. Place 4 cups dry couscous in a large bowl with 1 tbsp olive oil and ½ tsp salt.
  2. Pour 4 cups boiling water over it, stir once.
  3. Cover tightly and let sit for 5 minutes.
  4. Fluff with a fork. That's it.

Step 7: Serve

  1. Portion couscous and tofu-chickpea stew into containers.
  2. Garnish with fresh cilantro and a squeeze of lemon.
  3. For meal prep, keep couscous and stew together — the couscous absorbs sauce and becomes even more flavorful.

Meal Prep Notes

  • Reheating: Microwave 2-3 minutes. Add a tiny splash of water if couscous seems dry.
  • Day 2-3 is peak: The spices meld and deepen after sitting overnight.
  • Couscous absorbs sauce: This is a feature, not a bug. Portions get more flavorful each day.
  • Storage: Keeps well in the fridge for 5 days.

Notes

  • Bloom the spices. Toasting the spice blend in oil for a full minute before adding liquid is what separates a flat-tasting stew from one with real depth.
  • Dried apricots are doing a lot of work. They add natural sweetness and body as they break down. Don't skip them.
  • Green olives add salt and brininess. They balance the sweetness of the apricots and warmth of the cinnamon.
  • Couscous is foolproof. Equal parts couscous and boiling water, cover, wait 5 minutes, fluff.
  • No cornstarch needed. The crushed tomatoes and chickpeas thicken the sauce naturally.
