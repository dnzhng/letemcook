🥡 Sesame Ginger Tofu

with Coconut Rice

Week of February 2, 2026

Servings: 10
Prep time: 20 minutes
Cook time: 35 minutes
Total time: 55 minutes

Overview

Crispy baked tofu tossed in a sweet and savory sesame ginger glaze, served over creamy coconut rice with sautéed bok choy. A comforting, flavorful meal prep that's easy to make and keeps well all week.

Recipe adapted from various sesame tofu recipes with modifications for meal prep.

Ingredients

For the Tofu:

  • 3 blocks (42 oz total) extra-firm tofu
  • 3 tablespoons sesame oil
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste

For the Sesame Ginger Glaze:

  • ½ cup soy sauce
  • ¼ cup rice vinegar
  • ¼ cup honey
  • 3 tablespoons sesame oil
  • 2 tablespoons fresh ginger, minced
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon sriracha (optional, adjust to taste)
  • 2 tablespoons sesame seeds

For the Coconut Rice:

  • 4 cups jasmine rice
  • 2 cans (27 oz total) coconut milk
  • 2½ cups water
  • 1 teaspoon salt

For the Vegetables:

  • 1.5 lbs bok choy (about 4-5 heads)
  • 6 green onions

Instructions

Step 1: Prep the Tofu

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Press 3 blocks of extra-firm tofu for 15 minutes (use paper towels and place something heavy on top, or use a tofu press).
  3. Cut tofu into 1-inch cubes.
  4. Toss cubes with 3 tablespoons sesame oil, 3 tablespoons soy sauce, and a pinch of salt and pepper.
  5. Spread in a single layer on 2 lined baking sheets.
  6. Bake for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crispy on edges.

Step 2: Make the Coconut Rice

  1. Rinse 4 cups jasmine rice until water runs clear.
  2. In a large pot or rice cooker, combine:
    • 4 cups rinsed jasmine rice
    • 2 cans (27 oz) coconut milk
    • 2½ cups water
    • 1 teaspoon salt
  3. Bring to a boil, then reduce to low, cover, and simmer for 18 minutes.
  4. Remove from heat and let sit covered for 5 minutes.
  5. Fluff with a fork.

Step 3: Make the Sesame Ginger Glaze

  1. While rice cooks, whisk together in a small saucepan:
    • ½ cup soy sauce
    • ¼ cup rice vinegar
    • ¼ cup honey
    • 3 tablespoons sesame oil
    • 2 tablespoons minced fresh ginger
    • 6 cloves minced garlic
    • 1 tablespoon sriracha (optional)
  2. Bring to a simmer over medium heat and cook for 5-7 minutes until slightly thickened.
  3. Remove from heat and stir in 2 tablespoons sesame seeds.

Step 4: Prepare the Vegetables

  1. Wash and chop 1.5 lbs bok choy into 1-inch pieces, separating stems from leaves.
  2. Slice 6 green onions (white and green parts).
  3. In a large pan or wok over medium-high heat, add 1 tablespoon sesame oil (from your pantry).
  4. Sauté bok choy stems for 2 minutes.
  5. Add bok choy leaves and cook for 1-2 minutes until wilted.
  6. Season with a splash of soy sauce.

Step 5: Combine and Store

  1. In a large bowl or directly in meal prep containers, layer:
    • Coconut rice (bottom)
    • Sautéed bok choy
    • Crispy baked tofu
  2. Drizzle sesame ginger glaze over everything.
  3. Top with sliced green onions.
  4. Store in airtight containers in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.

Reheating Instructions

  • Microwave: Heat for 2-3 minutes, stirring halfway through.
  • Stovetop: Add a splash of water to a pan and heat over medium until warmed through.

Notes

  • For extra crispy tofu, you can air fry at 400°F for 15-18 minutes instead of baking.
  • Add a squeeze of lime juice when serving for extra brightness.
  • If you prefer more heat, increase sriracha or add chili flakes.
